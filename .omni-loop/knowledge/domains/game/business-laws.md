## BL-GAME-1
Merging a sub-PR earns its author no points by itself. The only credit is the bonus paid when the whole
PRD ships.
Why: Paying per sub-PR would reward cutting work thin instead of delivering it.

## BL-GAME-2
When a PRD is lost, every point earned on it is taken back in its season, including points for answers.
Why: Players would otherwise keep points for work that never reached anyone.

## BL-GAME-3
When a member leaves a workspace, their arcade player and high scores are removed with them.
Why: A team's score table would keep showing someone who left, along with their GitHub identity.
