# Game principles

## P-GAME-1

In the game, players only ever join fleets of people; a virtual fleet is never one a player can be in.

Why: A virtual fleet holds agents, not crew, so a player in one would score and play under a fleet that no person belongs to.

## P-GAME-2

Only a workspace owner changes who crews a virtual fleet, and a fleet is never left without a hero.

Why: Members rely on a fleet's crew staying intact and changed only by those who answer for the workspace.
