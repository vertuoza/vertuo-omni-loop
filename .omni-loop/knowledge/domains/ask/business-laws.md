## BL-ASK-1
An answer a person gave (a reply, a review comment, an approval, or the words they typed into a
question) is never changed or reworded afterwards, by an agent or by the product. A new answer is added
beside it. What an agent writes in its own name (its status comments, PR descriptions, proofs and
outbox items) is not a person's answer, as long as it never speaks for that person.
Why: Every decision must trace back to the person's own words.
Also: approvals
