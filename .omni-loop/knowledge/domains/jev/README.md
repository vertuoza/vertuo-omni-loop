# Jev

Glossary term: Jev

Jev, the workspace's judge: its stored AI key, the questions a terminal asks it, and the record of how often it agrees.

Its files are the `jev` area of `.omni-loop/config.yml` (`flow.areas.jev`, `knowledge: jev`): `omni kb laws --paths` lists this folder's entries under **Applies here** for any file there (PRD 2346). An entry moved here from `product/` keeps its id, marked by its `Kept id:` line.
