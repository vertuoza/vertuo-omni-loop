## BL-JEV-1
Jev's answer changes what happens only for a decision the workspace has switched On, and only when Jev
answers at or above the confidence floor. Otherwise today's rules decide.
Why: A workspace that never agreed to let an AI decide would find it deciding its work.

## BL-JEV-2
When Jev fails, has no key or answers outside the options, the work carries on as it would without Jev.
Why: A team's delivery must not stop because its AI judge is down.
