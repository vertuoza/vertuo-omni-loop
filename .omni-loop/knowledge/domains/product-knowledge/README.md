# Product knowledge

Glossary term: Product knowledge

A product's knowledge on the Omni page: its Legal and Glossary entries, their owners and history, and the knowledge graph each repository pushes.

Its files are the `product-knowledge` area of `.omni-loop/config.yml` (`flow.areas.product-knowledge`, `knowledge: product-knowledge`): `omni kb laws --paths` lists this folder's entries under **Applies here** for any file there (PRD 2346). An entry moved here from `product/` keeps its id, marked by its `Kept id:` line.
