# Product knowledge principles

## P-PRODUCTKNOWLEDGE-1

A knowledge page always names who answers for it: the people who can actually change it where it is read from.

Why: Readers need to know who to ask about a page, and naming someone who cannot change it would send them to the wrong person.

## P-PRODUCTKNOWLEDGE-2

A product's knowledge shows as its repositories only those the product actually links, never one it merely draws pages from.

Why: Listing an unlinked repository as the product's own would mislead people about what the product is made of.
