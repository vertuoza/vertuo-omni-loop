What stays the same

The repository is the source of truthThe spec, the page and the plan are still written, reviewed and merged as files. The dossier keeps copies and their history.
The phase-0 PRIt is still docs-only, reviewed and merged by a person before any code is written.
Ask mode's rulesQuestions are recorded only while ask mode is on, and nothing ever blocks a session.
Who readsMembers of the workspace, as with PRD 144's history. Nobody outside it, and no public link.
What leaves the machineThe three files, which go to GitHub anyway, plus the repository, the PRD number, the title and the Claude session id. Never transcript text.
Off means offWith dossier.enabled: false, nothing is uploaded and the fallback skips the repository. What is stored stays readable.
