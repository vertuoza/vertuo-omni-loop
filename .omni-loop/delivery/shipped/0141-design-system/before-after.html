New · logo

The logo, in three forms

The 16-bit crest. Every O is a loop arrow: a pixel ring broken at the top right, its end sharpened into an arrowhead. The letters are a chunky pixel face. Both are finished by the sprite forge's own rules: a 4-tone ramp from pale yellow to ember, lit from the top left, a navy outline and a plasma drop shadow. The geometry here is the sketch the logo slice starts from; it draws the final pixels.

Tokens · colour

Today's colours, plus six for the logo and the ad

Every existing colour keeps its value. The ones marked new finish the logo's ramp (highlight at the top, ember at the bottom, with the existing yellow and orange between) and give the print ad its voice: a hot magenta, the ad's purple text column, and the starfield behind the hero art.

New · sprites

OmniMan learns to point

Three poses on the same 32×48 body, drawn through the forge and recolourable like every hero. The poster scale renders any sprite at up to 16×, on the pixel grid. These are silhouettes: the final art is drawn in the slice.
