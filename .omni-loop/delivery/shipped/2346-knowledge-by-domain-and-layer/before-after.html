galaxy

Functional

Today

On a product's Repositories tab, an owner types any word as a repository's role: back-end, api, backend. Two repositories doing the same job can carry two words.

After

The owner picks the role from nine: api, web, mobile, ui, library, infra, plan, doc, monorepo. A role saved before that the list does not hold stays, shown as not a role until the owner picks one.

supabase

Functional

Today

A product's repository role is any kebab-case word of up to 40 characters.

After

Known old words become their role once (back-end → api, front-end → web, docs → doc); a new role must be one of the nine; any other old word is kept as it is.

Deploy order

Behaviour

Today
  • kb laws --paths prints every locked law, then the rules whose Governs: matches.
  • omni check knowledge refuses a locked law with no Governs:.
  • /omni:invade proposes no domain, area or layer.
After
  • It prints what applies (by Governs:, domain or layer), then what is global, then a count of what is hidden.
  • A law with no Governs: is accepted: reachable by its domain or layers, or global.
  • /omni:invade --refresh proposes layers, knowledge areas, domain folders (ids kept) and ADR layers.
