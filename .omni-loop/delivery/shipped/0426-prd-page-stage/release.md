title: The PRD page shows its stage and what to do next
---
A PRD's page now shows where it stands, from idea to retro, with one button for what to do next:
approve the spec, answer the outbox, review and merge, or read the retro. "PRD #n" links to its
issue, and new Outbox and Retro tabs show the decisions and the retro.
