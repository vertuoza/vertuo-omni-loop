galaxy

Functional

Today

A PRD's approver reads the spec and approves. Nothing checks the spec against the product's business rules, and on the PRD page no canon check runs at all.

Knowledge lists Legal entries, the glossary and domains. The product's business rules are nowhere on the page.

After

The PRD's spec is judged against the laws its domains and glossary words point to. A break turns the approval off, and shows the law, why it exists, the spec's words and who owns it.

Knowledge gains a read-only Business laws section. A domain's page lists its laws. Changing a law stays a pull request the PM approves.

Today

kit

Functional

Today

An agent reads a repository's rules, and its locked laws, for the files it changes. A rule the whole product shares has no home.

Changing a repository's locked law needs its CODEOWNER.

After

An agent also reads the business laws of the domains it touches. A PM can run omni kb business-laws --spec to see what a spec will be judged against. The brainstorm names those laws in the spec.

Changing a business law needs the PM, as a CODEOWNER of its file. An agent can draft the change and never merges it.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. The migration: business_law_verdicts and its writer, with dossier_approve refusing a red. No verdict exists yet, so nothing changes.
  2. apps/galaxy: the judge, the route, the page.
  3. apps/omni-app: the sixth gate and law-locked on the files. It calls the route, and reads a refusal as neutral.
  4. The kit release: the check, the commands and the skills.
