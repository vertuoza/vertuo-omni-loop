PRD 94 · Before and after · apps/galaxy

A Game Boy on phones, the screen alone on desktop

On a phone the arcade becomes a Game Boy you hold upright, with a near-square screen whose text reads at its designed size, and a D-pad, A, B, SELECT and START under your thumbs. Sideways it becomes a Game Boy Advance. On a computer, where the keyboard is the controller, the frame is gone and the screen fills the window, fullscreen from the first key.

Every device below is drawn to scale from the real viewport: a unit is one CSS pixel of the phone or the window, and the screen inside it is scaled exactly as the arcade scales it. The numbers on the menus are examples.

How the controls behave

Today every control fires on release, once. After, the D-pad reads the thumb like a rocker, and a held direction repeats the way a held arrow key does.

  • Touch-down, not releaseControls fire on pointerdown. Today they wait for click.
  • Several fingersEach finger is its own press: hold ▶ and tap A at the same time.
  • A buzzA 10 ms vibration per press where the browser offers one (Android). iPhone Safari stays silent.
  • The page holds stillNo scroll, pinch, double-tap zoom, pull-to-refresh, text selection or long-press menu on the Game Boy.
  • Fullscreen on touchThe first press asks for it where the browser allows (Chrome on Android, iPad). An iPhone keeps Safari's bars.
  • Keyboards still workEvery key does what it does today, in every form, so a Bluetooth keyboard drives the Game Boy too.

The hints name the buttons you have

The arcade's rule is that the keys a screen shows are the keys to press. On the Game Boy, those are its buttons. On a computer, the hints stay as they are.

What stays the same

This PRD changes what surrounds the screen and how scenes are laid out on it. The game underneath is untouched.

  • The game's rules, points, decay and rankings.
  • The galaxy's data, the ledger and Supabase: no migration, no new stored value.
  • Signing in with Google, linking GitHub, and who may see what.
  • Every scene and what it shows. A tall layout rearranges and never drops.
  • The keyboard's keys, and keys.test.ts, unchanged.
  • The music and the sounds, and the omni-loop:muted setting.
  • Deep links: #map, #fleets, #heroes, #briefing, #planet-….
  • The single-file artifact, which gets the same forms.
