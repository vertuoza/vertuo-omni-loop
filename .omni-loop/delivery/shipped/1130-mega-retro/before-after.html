What stays the same

  • A one-repository PRD's retro reads and writes exactly what it does today.
  • The retro, its issues and its PR live in the plan repository. In a target, the App writes one comment and nothing else.
  • A merge in a target starts no retro. The knowledge PR is unchanged.
