PRD 2482: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

A job waits on Queued until a runner takes it. When no runner of the workspace can take it (none holds its repository, the runner or fleet it went to is gone, or it needs its API key's GitHub App and no runner can run one), it still reads Queued, forever. Nothing on the page says why.

On 2026-10-23, the Omni app's retro jobs for four repositories sat on Queued: the workspace's one runner held none of them.

After

That job reads Cannot run, in red, with the reason under it:

  • No runner holds nimbus/nimbus-work; for a job sent to one runner, <runner> does not hold …; to a fleet, No runner of <fleet> holds …
  • The runner it was sent to is gone, or Fleet <fleet> is gone
  • No runner can run it as <key>'s GitHub App

A runner that is offline, busy or paused still counts: its jobs stay Queued (a paused one keeps its Waiting line). Nothing is stored: Cancel stays, and once a runner that can take the job registers, the row reads Queued again within 5 seconds and the job runs.
