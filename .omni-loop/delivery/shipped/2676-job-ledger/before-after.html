PRD 2676: before and after

omni-app

Functional

Today

Knowledge merges. The pack is made by a job on Inngest. The account is over its free limit, so no job runs and no pack is made. A spec reads: Business laws not judged: the law home has no knowledge pack yet. Nobody is told.

After

A knowledge merge adds a job to our own ledger, and the pack follows within a minute. If the job fails or the machine dies it is tried again by itself. A webhook that never arrived is noticed within the hour. If it still jams, an issue opens on GitHub.

Behaviour

Today

A merge sends the harvest event, and Inngest runs the pack. When Inngest stops, the pack stops.

After

A merge writes a ledger row first; the harvest event still goes to Inngest. ok on /api/health turns false when jobs are late, which opens the health issue.
