omni runner cleans up after itself

PRD 1911. What a runner leaves on its server after its jobs, today and after.

Functional

Today 
# after one day on vertuo-agents
$ git branch --list "agent/*"
  agent/job-631c499f…
  agent/job-7402e63d…
  agent/job-c1c83187…   … 6 in all
$ git worktree list
  …/wt/…-job-d0fe60a3…/.claude/worktrees/pr-care-1737  prunable
$ ls ~/logs
  job-*.log  job-*.line  job-*.exit   kept forever

A job ends: its worktree goes, its branch, records and logs stay. A worktree whose job died with the runner stays too.

After 
# a job ends
  agent/job-<id> deleted (never pushed) · worktree records pruned

# at start, then every hour
swept: 3 worktrees, 6 branches, 12 log files · 412 MB freed

# kept, always
  a pushed agent/job-* branch
  everything of a running job
  ~/logs/runner.log, anything else in ~/wt and ~/logs
  job logs younger than --keep-logs (14 days)

Only ~/wt/<repo>-job-<id>, ~/logs/job-<id>.* and local agent/job-* branches are ever removed.
