PRD 1572 · before / after

OMNI KART races SINGLE or MULTI

The ready screen offers SINGLE, today's race against five CPU karts, or MULTI: the workspace's grid, where up to four players race COMET RING live, each from their own browser. The best time takes pole, BLOBs and ORBs hit the other players, and a MULTI time counts for the best time. Names below are examples.

The others keep racing · a hidden tab leaves

  • Each browser drives its own kart and sends where it is; the others' karts are drawn from it.
  • BLOBs and ORBs reach the other players; the player hit decides the hit, and the item leaves every circuit.
  • No pause: the menu opens while the race goes on. A hidden tab leaves the race, and a player silent for 10 s is OUT.

Under the hood

The channel
your browser                Supabase Realtime          a colleague's browser
                            private · kart:<workspace>
presence: who, best time ─▶ presence ───────────────▶ the grid, in order
START: seed, order       ─▶ broadcast ──────────────▶ 3 · 2 · 1 · GO
your kart's state        ─▶ broadcast ──────────────▶ your kart, carried forward
BLOB / ORB               ─▶ broadcast ──────────────▶ on their circuit
                         ◀─ hit ◀──────────────────── they decide they were hit
finish ─▶ RESULTS · submit_score(time), unchanged

No server and no host: each browser owns its own kart. A migration lets only a player of the workspace with OMNI KART unlocked join the topic, with their own session.
