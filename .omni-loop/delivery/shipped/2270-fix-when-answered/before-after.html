PRD 2270 · A runner job waiting on answers fixes by itself once they are in

Today a job that stopped on questions reads Waiting on answers forever, even once every question is answered. After, the answers start the fix on the same runner, once, and the row says so; a per-repository switch keeps a person in charge.

Functional

Today

  1. The runner ends /omni:yolo 271 on Waiting on answers.
  2. You answer both questions, on GitHub or on the PRD page.
  3. The row still reads Waiting on answers; nothing runs until someone sends /omni:yolo-fix 271.

After

  1. The runner ends /omni:yolo 271 on Waiting on answers, as today.
  2. Your last answer is a comment: the page reads the PRD again and sees every high question answered.
  3. It queues /omni:yolo-fix 271 once, to the same runner, in your name; the row reads Answered · fixing.
  4. Switch off: the row reads Answered with Fix with answers, and nothing starts until you press it.
