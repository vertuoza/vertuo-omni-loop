PRD 2425: before and after — an API key follows its job

galaxy

Functional

TODAY
  • An app that sent a bug fix with its oma_ key polls GET /api/v1/jobs/{id}, and every stop reads the same: status: done, pr_url: null, whether the bug was already fixed, was not a bug, or waits on a decision.
  • At most one pull request, of the job's own repository; a mega-bug-fix's targets and a failed job's draft are not there.
  • The job's lines, its questions, Cancel, Send again and Resume take a person on the Runners page.
  • Once a person answers or relaunches it, the new job is that person's: the key gets 404 on it.
  • Nothing tells the app a job changed: it asks again every few seconds.
AFTER
  • The job says how it ended: outcome (already_fixed, pr_opened, need_human, needs_brainstorm, not_a_bug, stuck, blocked), fixed_by, and every pull request in prs.
  • With the key, the app reads the job's lines and log tail, reads its questions and answers or dismisses them, cancels or relaunches it.
  • Every job that carries the work on stays the key's, linked by continues and continued_by; any key of the workspace follows it, within its allow-list.
  • Each action may say whom the app acted for (acting_for, free text). The trace is the key's, never a person's.
  • With notify_url, the server pings the app when the job changes: {job_id, event, at}, nothing else.
  • On the Runners page, members see which key acted, and for whom.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. supabase: the migration <version>_api_key_job_control.sql (the columns, the widened outcome check, runner_job_answer and the relaunch keeping the key), applied by the supabase workflow on merge, with its check supabase/checks/api_key_jobs.sql.
  2. galaxy: the routes, the report's new fields, the ping and the page's attribution, deployed by the release that follows. It must run before any runner takes the new kit: JobUpdateSchema is strict, so a galaxy that does not know fixedBy, prs or the new outcome words refuses that runner's ending report.
  3. kit: the release v0.0.N with the skills' new lines and the runner reading them; each runner takes it with omni update. A runner on an older kit keeps working and reports no outcome.
