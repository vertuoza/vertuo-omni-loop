Archive a product

PRD 2232. A workspace owner archives a product: it leaves every list, no endpoint answers for it, and nothing is erased. Restore brings it back as it was. Drawn from the spec (spec.md) and from today's code (apps/galaxy/src/products/, apps/galaxy/src/product-home/), in the app's Omni theme. The figures are examples.

Functional

Who. A workspace owner archives and restores, as for the product's repositories and key (product_repository_owned()). A member is refused with 42501 and sees no Archive, no Archived line and no Restore.

WhatAfter

Archive / Restoreproduct_archive(p_product) sets both; product_restore(p_product) clears both. Each answers {id, name, archivedAt}. Every row under the product stays.
Members' listlive products only; an owner also reads the archived ones
Kit and agentsevery lookup skips it, owners included; a named product answers P0002 Product <name> is archived: an owner restores it on its page. Its repositories resolve to their other products, or none, until restore.
ChangesLegal, glossary, links, approvers, key, dossier, roadmap and bug pushes refuse it with that P0002
