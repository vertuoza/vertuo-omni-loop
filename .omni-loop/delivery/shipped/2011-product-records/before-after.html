PRD 2011 · Roadmaps, bugs and knowledge move into the product

From concept #1269, area product-records. Once a repository reaches step 5 of the rollout ladder, its new roadmaps and bug records are born on the server, in its product, and imported knowledge is served as packs. The plan repository stays, and the old way keeps working beside the new one. Everything stays visible across products in one place: the Work pages list every product, with a product filter.

Two parts: Functional (the flows, today beside after) and Pixel perfect (the screens). Screens: questions · prd-dossier · bug-record · product-repositories · app-shell.

Functional

Today a roadmap is a git file reviewed in a phase-0 PR, copied to the server on every push. After, it is born on its product, versioned, and approved on its page.

Imported knowledge

Today an imported target's knowledge is a copy in the plan repository. After, the server snapshots it on merge, and an agent fetches the pack at the link's read_at.

Today

/omni:mega-invade --sync
→ knowledge/repos/nimbus-web/ (copy)
→ knowledge/repos/nimbus-web/flow/ (copy)
/omni:do-work reads the copy · may be weeks old

After

web#220 merged, touches .omni-loop/knowledge/ → pack 92c04aa
omni kb pack get nimbus-web

server down → held: newest fetched pack 92c04aa · 2 h old
pack older than read_at → pack stale: 81aa < read_at 92c0 · slice stopped

What stays the same

A record born in git finishes in git: its roadmap.md, its issue, its bug.md and its record PR work as today. A repository below step 5 changes nothing. Visual fixes are untouched. Nothing here merges into a default branch.
