PRD 3233: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

The fleets of agents sit in Dashboard › Fleet, in the same picker as the players' fleets, marked “· virtual”. Their tabs (Operation room, Missions, Heroes, Board, Commander) never say where their work runs. To find out, a member opens Runners, looks for the fleet's name in the runner list, then for the PRD in the job list, and matches the two by hand. Work that went to a Shared runner, one with no fleet, shows nowhere as the fleet's.

After

AI Fleet is an entry of the main menu, right under Inbox. It opens on the list of the workspace's AI fleets: for each, its heroes, its mission and how far it is, what waits on you, and the runners it runs on, Shared ones marked. New AI fleet lives here.

Opening a fleet shows its tabs as today, plus Runners, and a Runs on line under its name on every tab. Runners lists its own runners, the Shared runners running its work and why, and the jobs waiting for a slot and why: each job with its role, its PRD, its status and how long. A job waiting on you has Answer. When something is off (no runner of its own, none in the workspace, offline, every slot busy, out of tokens, a job no runner can take) the tab says so in the Runners page's words.

Fleet keeps only the players' fleets of the season. An old link to an AI fleet on Fleet lands on its AI Fleet page. Nobody reads “Virtual Fleet” any more: it is “AI Fleet” on every screen.

Behaviour

Today

/app/fleet?fleet=<AI fleet> draws the AI fleet's tabs. The picker of /app/fleet lists AI fleets with “· virtual” and the New virtual fleet button. jobOf drops mission_id.

After

/app/fleet?fleet=<AI fleet> redirects to /app/ai-fleet?fleet=<name>, every other query kept. The picker of /app/fleet lists only players' fleets. jobOf keeps mission_id and adds the role from mission_key through missionJobRole, a copy of public.mission_job_role a test holds equal.
