PRD 2255 · A stopped bug fix asks for a human answer on its job, then resumes with it

Today a bug fix that needs a decision stops with a comment on its issue and reads Done on the Runners page: nobody knows, and the next step needs a terminal. After, the job reads Need human, the sidebar counts it, and anyone in the workspace answers in a drawer; the answer queues the fix again, on the same runner, with that answer.

Functional

Today

  1. vertuo-cleaner sends /omni:mega-bug-fix #314 for vertuo-automation-plan; a runner claims it.
  2. The agent finds the cause and three ways to fix it, but the right one is a decision about access.
  3. It posts a stop comment on #314 ending with /omni:mega-brainstorm …, and exits 0.
  4. The job reads Done, like a fix that worked. Nobody is told.
  5. The next step needs someone who reads the issue, opens a terminal and runs the line.

After

  1. Same run, same stop. The agent writes 1 to 4 questions (its fixes as options, plus Design it first).
  2. omni runner ask stores them on its job; the stop comment ends Waiting for an answer on Omni: <link>.
  3. The job reads Need human; the sidebar reads Runners 1. Nobody is pinged.
  4. Anyone opens the drawer, picks an option, presses Send answers: one transaction stores the answer and queues /omni:mega-bug-fix #314 --answers <job> on the same runner.
  5. The resume posts Decided in Omni by @irisa on #314, then fixes from its triage: red test, fix, PRs. A person merges.
