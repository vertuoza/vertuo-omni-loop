PRD #71 · before / after

Ask mode: Claude's questions on a page you can actually read

Turn it on with /omni:ask on. Every AskUserQuestion is then sent to a page in light or dark theme, you answer there, and Claude continues. If the page can't answer, the terminal prompt takes over as it does today.

One question in focus, in a font drawn for legibility. The galaxy's colours are used as signals: plasma for your pick, yellow for the recommendation, green for answered. Try the theme switch.

What changes

  • omni signin once per machine (Google, @vertuoza.com), then /omni:ask on|off per checkout.
  • While the mode is on, questions are answered on the page, and the terminal shows one line per question.
  • The galaxy app serves /ask/… pages and /api/ask/*. The kit only knows ask.url.
  • ADR-0002 lets the kit depend on that URL (amending PRD 3's principle 7).

What stays the same

  • With the mode off, every hook is silent and nothing differs from today.
  • No skill's text changes; the page shows exactly what AskUserQuestion carries.
  • The terminal always wins on failure: offline, signed out, or 9 min without an answer.
  • The arcade, the ledger and game points are untouched. Asking needs no player and no GitHub link.
