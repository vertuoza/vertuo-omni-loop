/omni:pixel-perfect is impeccable's critique, audit, polish, harden, typeset, layout, adapt and clarify, imported into the kit and tailored: no dependency to install. Anyone can type /omni:pixel-perfect polish the quote page. All of this is behind one config flag, design.enabled, off by default: a repository turns it on to opt in, and off to get today's loop back. Same for /omni:visual-fix: its real check runs the review against the variation the person picked. A repository with no screen keeps the form blank and never runs it. Imported from pbakaus/impeccable (Apache-2.0); the product's own design always wins over its craft floor.
