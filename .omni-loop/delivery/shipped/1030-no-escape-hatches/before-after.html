How we know nothing breaks

  • Every schema is tested on the module's fixtures, and refuses a missing column, a wrong type and a forbidden null.
  • pnpm schemas:verify --local parses every boundary's real read against the migrated, seeded database in CI.
  • pnpm schemas:verify --production does the same, read-only, against production once before ship.
  • pnpm galaxy:shots walks every arcade scene and /app with no page error.

What stays the same

  • Every page, command and output: a value that parses is the same value.
  • The database: no migration, no table or route change.
  • Tests, which may still cast their fixtures.
