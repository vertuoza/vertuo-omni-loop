PRD 1726 · before / after

Locked laws: a thesis, twenty laws a CODEOWNER owns, and only what a change touches

Today the knowledge base is 189 entries of equal weight, harvested after the fact, and an agent reads all of them or none. After, a junior reads one page, the twenty laws belong to a person, and an agent gets only the entries that govern its files.

An agent's PR changes a law's test. What happens?

Today

law-proof needs a high item, and any person may answer it. A knowledge or enforce PR is never blocked. No CODEOWNERS file exists.

After

law-locked on every kind of PR: red until a person CODEOWNERS lists for those files approves on GitHub, then green. An outbox item, a label or a typed trailer does not clear it. With no CODEOWNER, it stays red and names the law. The merge records Law-Change-Approved-By: @login.

A rule's test: unchanged, a high item any person answers.
