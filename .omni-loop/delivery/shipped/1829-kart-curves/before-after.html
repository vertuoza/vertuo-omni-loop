PRD 1829 · OMNI KART · COMET RING

Every corner a curve, the tunnel vaulted in a bend, the walkway harder

Every row shows COMET RING as it is today, after PRD 1775 and bug 1820, beside COMET RING after this PRD. The race, its rules, the items, MULTI and the best times stay; what changes is the shape of the road.

The circuit from above

Today
  • 64 × 64 tiles. Each of the 14 corners is a square block of 5 × 5 road tiles.
  • The racing line is a broken line, and every edge of the road runs north–south or east–west.
  • The tunnel is a straight box on the top leg; the walkway is 2 tiles wide, in line with the top straight.
After
boost pad ramp tunnel walkway shortcut gate
  • 72 × 72 tiles, the same order and the same shape; the short legs grow so every curve fits, and the whole is centred on the map.
  • Each corner is an arc of its own radius, in tiles: 8 at the end of the start straight, 3 in the chicane and its exits, 5 in the tunnel, 6 on the wide curves, 4 in the U-loop.
  • The lap on the centre line is about 249 tiles, against 236 on today's broken line: about 5 % longer.
  • The pads, the ramps, the gap jump, the bump jump and the hidden ramp keep their sizes and their order, moved with their legs. The four boxes before the corner of the tunnel move to the straight after its exit; 40 boxes, 6 places and 12 stands, as today.

A corner, up close

Today
  • The road is a square block, the waypoint at its centre.
  • A kart at the top speed turns on a circle of about 4.4 tiles (steer × steerAtTop, 1.56 radians a second at 110), so it already drives round, across the block: every corner is taken flat out.
  • The rivals aim along the broken line.
After
  • The road is round: an outer and an inner quarter circle, 5 tiles apart. A kart tips over past the round edge as past a straight one, with the rules of PRD 1447 and PRD 1775.
  • A curve of 5 tiles or more is taken flat out; on the centre line, a hairpin of 3 asks to lift to about 0.78 of the top speed, a curve of 4 to about 0.85.
  • The racing line follows the arcs, sampled every half tile. A waypoint is passed across the dotted line, at the middle of its arc.

The tunnel: a vault, in the bend after the gap jump

Today
  • A straight box of 13 tiles on the top leg, after the landing and the corner.
  • A flat ceiling at ROOF 44 over the whole road (the eye is at 28); the exit portal is in sight from the entry.
After
  • In the bend: 13 tiles along the centre line, from where the curve of waypoint 5 starts: its quarter circle (about 8 tiles), then about 5 tiles of straight west. A kart lands from the gap jump and drives straight in.
  • The walls stand on the road's edges and follow the curve; the outer wall hides the exit until the kart is in the bend.
  • The vault: ROOF 44 over the centre line, falling along an arc to ROOF − RULES.vault at the walls, still above the eye. The panels and the strips of light follow it; its depth is set by the plan.
  • Portals square to the centre line at both ends; from inside, the road stays in sight (bug 1813). At most 600 samples a column, in the curve and on its straight.

The walkway: one tile, off the line, with a gap

Today
  • 2 tiles wide for a kart drawn 14 units wide, in line with the top straight: a kart lines it up on the straight and drives across.
  • Plain road to the wheels: it skips the whole U-loop, and two waypoints, at no risk.
After

The flights are drawn with the jump's own numbers (launch 0.9, gravity 400, 4 px a unit); the gap's length is calibrated by the plan.

  • One tile wide (16 units for a kart 14 wide): a kart that drifts half a tile off its line tips over.
  • Off the line: it leaves the curve of waypoint 6 at the middle of its arc, about 1.8 tiles south of the top straight's axis. It cannot be lined up on the straight, only taken out of the curve, with a flick of the wheel. No rail and no straight lead to it.
  • A ramp, then a gap, in its middle. Flat out on its line without a BOOST, a kart clears it; at 0.9 × the top speed it falls in and comes back on the racing line, in the U-loop.
  • Its gate keeps its place where it rejoins, and still credits waypoints 7 and 8 to a kart that crossed it forwards.
