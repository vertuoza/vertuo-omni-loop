When a retro does teach something

One issue per finding, up to five, whether or not it holds a lesson.

For example, a check red on several commits whose cause no knowledge entry names. retro.md still lists every finding. Issues open only for kept findings.

One rule for the look, shared by the harvest and the retro

An entry states what the product does and guarantees, never how it looks: no colour, size, layout, position, count of visual elements, font, or exact label or copy. A candidate that is only about the look stays local. A candidate that mixes both is written as the behaviour alone.

Forward only: entries already in the knowledge base are left as they are.
