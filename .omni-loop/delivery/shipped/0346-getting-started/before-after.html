PRD 346: Getting Started docs, and one top bar

Today, nothing tells a newcomer on a blank laptop how to install the kit, invade a repository and run a first PRD. After this PRD, the app carries that guide at /docs. HOME's footer points to it, and every page of the app reaches it, along with Release notes, from one shared top bar.

1 · HOME, the "Join the loop!" footer

The only change on HOME is a third button in the footer block. The poster at the top stays the same.

2 · The app, /app

Release notes stop being one of the person's cards. They become a menu item in one top bar, shared by /app, /releases, /prd, /ask, /knowledge and /docs.

3 · The docs, /docs

The pages are markdown files in docs/guide/, rendered with Fumadocs inside the app in its own look. They are public, so no sign-in is needed.

What stays the same

  • HOME's poster, its spreads and the arcade at /play.
  • /releases itself, and the pipeline that syncs shipped PRDs into it.
  • The kit and the plugin. No command or skill changes. The docs only describe them, and a test fails if they name one that does not exist.
  • The database. No migration.
