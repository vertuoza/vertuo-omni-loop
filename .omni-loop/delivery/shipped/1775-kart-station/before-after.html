PRD 1775 · OMNI KART · COMET RING

A station to drive through, jumps, pads, shortcuts, and a fairer edge

Every row shows COMET RING today beside COMET RING after this PRD. The race, the rivals, the boxes, the relief and the best times stay; what changes is how the lap rides and what stands around it.

The edge

Today
  • Both wheels of one side over the void tip the kart at once, when its centre is only 3 units past the edge (the kart is 14 wide).
  • A wheel over the void draws the kart towards the edge at 16 units a second.
After
  • Both wheels of one side out start a count: the kart falls only after 0.5 s in a row; one wheel back on the road resets it.
  • While it counts, the kart leans fully and rocks (no rocking with reduced motion).
  • The pull drops to 8 units a second. A centre 7 units past the edge still falls at once.

The circuit: pads, jumps, shortcuts, a tunnel

Today
  • One way around, on the ground all the way.
  • The BOOST only comes from an item box.
After
boost pad ramp tunnel walkway stand (a fleet's ship) shortcut gate
  • Pads give the item's BOOST (×1.4 for 1.5 s) and keep the item in hand: one before each ramp of the main line, one out of the corners [18, 56], [44, 28] and [8, 40].
  • The gap jump in the descent [56, 28] → [56, 8], the bump jump on the west leg.
  • The hidden ramp at [54, 40] and the walkway at [36, 8] each skip two waypoints; their gate credits both, so the lap counts. Rivals never take them.
  • The tunnel, 13 tiles, on the top leg, right after the gap jump.

The gap jump, seen from the side

Today
  • The kart rides the ground down the hump; it never leaves it.
After
  • Leaving a ramp, a kart takes off at 0.9 × its speed upwards, under a gravity of 400. In the air its speed holds, it steers at 0.3, takes no box or pad and cannot tip.
  • It lands with the ordinary rules: road, drive on; centre over the void, today's fall and way back. The camera rises with the player; a shadow stays on the ground.
  • The hidden ramp's gap is 5 tiles, landing 8 units higher: only an item's BOOST clears it.

The top leg: open road, then a space station

Today
  • An open road over the void, with the sky above.
After
  • A roof 44 units over the road (the eye is at 28): the top of the screen is the ceiling, with strips of light passing overhead.
  • Walls of metal panels with portholes on the stars; the road is station plating; a lit portal at each end.
  • From outside: a station block, its roof seen from a hill. Anything behind a wall is hidden. The floor still takes at most 600 samples a column.

The start straight: the fleets watch

Today
  • Pylons, beacons, a few asteroids, satellites and wrecks; two small planets and a station far off.
After
  • Twelve stands (eight along the start straight, four by the gap jump) hold the active fleets' ships, each in its mascot and colour, cycling; the neutral ship when there is no fleet. They bob, and stand still with reduced motion.
  • The roadside: neon lamps that pulse, antenna dishes that turn, docking cranes, cargo containers, on the void, more of them around the station.
  • The sky: convoys crossing, an asteroid belt, a big planet rising at the horizon. The road: cyan chevron pads, yellow arrows before the ramps, striped ramps, the walkway's magenta-edged grating.
