PRD 1359 · before / after

OMNI KART

An original kart racer in the SNES Mode 7 style. It unlocks at LV 3 in the game room's third cabinet. You race your own hero over three laps of COMET RING against five karts driven by your workspace's fleet mascots, with BOOST, BLOB and ORB items. It runs on its own engine, loads only when you open the cabinet, and uses no Nintendo assets.

The game room, at LV 2

Today

The third cabinet says SOON. Reaching LV 3 opens nothing.

After

The third cabinet is OMNI KART. Below LV 3, A on it answers REACH LV 3 TO PLAY. From LV 3 it lights up with the crew's top 5 and A · PLAY, like the other two.

The race

Today
No racing game in the arcade.
After · wide grid 640×360
  • The floor is COMET RING's texture projected in perspective from behind your kart, turning with it. The sky is the Omni galaxy and scrolls as you turn.
  • ◀ ▶ steer, A held accelerates, ▼ brakes, B uses the item, START pauses.
  • Item boxes give one item: BOOST, BLOB (an Entropy blob dropped behind you) or ORB (thrown straight ahead). Driving over a BLOB or taking an ORB means a 1-second spin-out.
  • Five rivals, one per fleet of your workspace, each driven by its mascot. You start last.
  • On the phone's handheld it plays the same race on the tall grid, 320×288.

The finish

Today
Nothing to finish, no score under kart.
After
  • Place points (1000, 700, 500, 350, 200, 100) plus a point per tenth of a second under the 150-second par. In this example, 2:22.0 is 8 seconds under par, so +80.
  • Rivals still racing are placed by how far along they are, with -- for a time.
  • The score is sent once under kart, with the same SAVING SCORE…, NEW BEST, YOUR BEST and SCORE NOT SAVED (A to retry) as the other games.
  • Quitting from the pause screen before the finish sends nothing.
