PRD 3001: before and after

kit

Functional

Today
  • A mission's QA seat has skills, but no skill that checks a PRD and hands back a verdict.
  • /omni:prove films the criteria once a person asks; nothing tries what they do not name.
After
  • /omni:qa <n> checks each acceptance criterion on the preview, then tries its edges.
  • Each bug, five at most, becomes an issue with its steps, what was expected and what happened.
  • It hands back pass or bugs, round 1 or 2, and never writes the branch.

omni-app

Functional

Today
  • A mission's PRD goes: built, then the Reviewer, then the fix, then the Reviewer again.
  • Nobody opens the preview before a person does.
After
  • Built, then QA, then the Engineer fixes each bug one at a time, then QA once more, then the Reviewer.
  • A pass in QA's first round goes straight to the Reviewer.
  • A bug still there after two rounds waits for you: Send on to the Reviewer or Stop this PRD.

galaxy

Functional

Today
  • The Operation room has no QA line and no QA row; a job row never says what it cost.
  • Heroes: QA runs 2 of 3 skills alone, and "reports what it saw; a person decides".
After
  • The room shows QA's line and its rows: bugs found, fixed, pass, with the round and the issues.
  • Every job row ends with its tokens, or "tokens unknown".
  • Heroes: QA runs 3 of 4 skills alone, never writes the branch, reports five bugs and checks two rounds at most.

database

Functional

Today
  • A runner job keeps its status and times, not what it spent.
After
  • A runner job also keeps its tokens, sent by the runner when it ends.
  • A QA verdict is a mission event, like the Reviewer's.

Flow

Deploy order

  • 1. The migration 20270125150000_qa_turn.sql (supabase workflow): runner_jobs.tokens, the checked event kind, QA's defaults in virtual_hero_roles(). All additive.
  • 2. The kit: /omni:qa, the verdict module, qa in the runner's list, the runner sending its tokens. Runners pick it up at their next kit pull.
  • 3. galaxy and omni-app (Vercel), together with the feature PR: the job end stores tokens, the supervisor sends the QA pair, the room and Heroes show it.
  • Rollback: revert the feature PR; the column is nullable and the event kind only added, so the old code reads the database as it is.
