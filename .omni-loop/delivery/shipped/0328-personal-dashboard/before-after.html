What stays the same

  • / (HOME) and the arcade at /play. The arcade's APP MODE row and GAME ▮▯ APP switch still land on /app.
  • The app bar: the OMNI LOOP · App mark, the Omni, Light and Dark themes, and Game mode.
  • The sections themselves (/ask, /ask/for-me, /ask/history, /knowledge, /releases). Only their cards on /app get smaller.
  • The ledger and the game's scoring. The dashboard adds no points, no XP and no ledger event.
  • The season: the calendar month the arcade already scores.
