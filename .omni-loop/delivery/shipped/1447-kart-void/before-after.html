PRD 1447 · OMNI KART

COMET RING floats in the void

Everything off the road becomes space. A kart whose centre goes over the edge falls for one second, then comes back at rest in the middle of the road, at the point of the racing line nearest where it fell. The clock keeps running, the lap counter never moves, and the rivals follow the same rule.

The circuit from above

Today

Wide in the corner, the kart slows on the verge, scrapes the wall and bounces back. In space, going off the road costs little and shows nothing.

After

No verge, no wall: the road hangs over a starfield, its neon edge is the edge of the void, and the pylons and asteroids stay where they stood, floating. The sky and the horizon do not change.

Missing the corner

Today

The kart scrapes the wall: a scrape sound, a few sparks, and it drives on at half speed until it is back on the road.

After

1 s falling + the restart from rest ≈ 1.5 s lost. The clock runs, the lap counter does not move. A BOOST in progress ends; the item held stays.

Where the kart comes back

Today

There is no way back to choose: the wall stops the kart, and it drives back from the verge on its own.

After

The way back is on the segment the kart is racing, from the last waypoint it passed (A) to the next one (B): the nearest point to where it fell, and never past B, nor across the start line. Its laps and waypoints are kept exactly as they were.

What changes, what stays

Changes
  • The verge and the walls become the void, on the map and on the floor.
  • A kart over the void falls for 1 s, then comes back on the racing line and blinks 0.5 s.
  • Rivals fall and come back by the same rule; a push from another kart can make them fall.
  • A falling kart is not pushed, not hit, takes no box and uses no item.
  • The fall sound replaces scrape; the wall's sparks go.
  • An ORB turns back at the road's edge instead of at a wall.
Stays
  • The road's layout, its kerbs, boxes, start line and racing line.
  • The sky and the horizon; the scenery beyond it is its own PRD.
  • The race time sent at the finish, and every best time already kept.
  • BOOST, BLOB, ORB and the spin-out keep their rules.
  • A whole simulated race proves each rival still finishes its three laps, with at most one fall.
