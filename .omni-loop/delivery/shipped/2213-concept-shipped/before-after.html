PRD 2213: A concept reads as shipped

Today beside after. A concept gains a fourth state, shipped: by itself once every area's PRD shipped, or by a person marking it done.

Functional

Today

The conceptReads
Its concept PR is openin review
Its concept PR mergedin the inbox, for good
No facts stored, or the PR unread or missingstate unknown

Once merged, the sync never reads the concept again: a closed issue is never seen.

After

The conceptReads
Its concept PR is open (its issue's state does not count)in review
Merged, every area names a PRD, each PRD at shipped or retroshipped (by itself)
Merged, its issue closed: Mark done on the page, or closed on GitHubshipped (by a person)
Merged, otherwisein the inbox
No facts stored, or the PR unread or missingstate unknown

The sync keeps reading a merged concept's issue, one read per run; an issues event refreshes it at once.

Mark done, end to end

A member presses Mark done→ POST /api/concepts/done, checked as the caller (is_member)→ the Omni app closes the issue as completed, with "Marked done on the Omni page by @login."→ the concept's facts are stored→ the page reloads: shipped

Reopen runs the same way and reopens the issue. A refused caller or a failed GitHub call changes nothing and says so in one line.
