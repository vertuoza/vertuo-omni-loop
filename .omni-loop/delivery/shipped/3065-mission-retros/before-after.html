PRD 3065: before and after

database

Functional

Today

A mission keeps its repairs (one per failed step), its budget stops and its events. Nothing records a mission that goes nowhere, a milestone's lessons, or a proposed rule change.

After

A repair also records why it started: a failed step, or a stuck mission. A mission keeps its milestone retros and its own questions (proposed rule changes), each answered once and kept for good.

omni-app

Functional

Today

The supervisor repairs a failed step (PRD 2980) and pauses at a token cap (PRD 3040). A mission whose jobs keep ending without any PRD getting further runs on until a person looks. A delivered roadmap or mission ends with no look back.

After

After 5 jobs in a row where nothing moved, the supervisor sends one stuck repair. It fixes the cause, retries a flake, reports an Omni Loop bug, or pauses the mission and asks once. When a roadmap of the mission, or the mission, is delivered, it sends one milestone retro. Every retro counts toward the repair budget and the token caps.

kit

Functional

Today

/omni:repair repairs one failed job. No skill writes a mission's lessons, and a lesson about a rule has nowhere to go but a PRD's outbox.

After

/omni:repair --stuck reads the last 5 jobs instead of one. A new /omni:mission-retro writes what went well and what to change, each sentence citing what happened. A rule it thinks is wrong becomes a question in the mission's own outbox; it never changes the rule.

galaxy

Functional

Today

The mission page shows a mission running, waiting, paused (by a person or a budget) or delivered. The Operation room lists repairs and budget stops.

After

The mission page adds Stuck: 5 jobs, nothing moved, Paused: stuck with Resume and Stop, a Retros list and a Questions list with Adopt and Decline. Adopt records the answer and says how to apply it. The room adds a row per retro and per question.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. database: 20270126110000_mission_retros.sql, additive (a column with a default, two tables, a function, five event kinds).
  2. kit: /omni:repair --stuck, /omni:mission-retro, the two schemas; runners pull the kit.
  3. omni-app: the stuck counter and the milestone trigger in the supervisor.
  4. galaxy: the mission page and the room.

All in one feature PR; the migration ships first with it. Nothing acts until a mission runs.

Behaviour

Today

outcome() sends a repair on a failure, a second hold or a second stop mid-way. Nothing counts jobs that moved nothing; a delivered mission sends nothing more.

After

stuckNext() (stuck.ts) counts ended jobs since something moved and sends a stuck repair at 5; milestoneNext() (milestone.ts) sends one retro per delivered roadmap and one for the mission; both wait while a cap holds the mission.
