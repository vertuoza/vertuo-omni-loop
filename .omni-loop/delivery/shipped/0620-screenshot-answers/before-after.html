The hook downloads each file inside the checkout (never committed; cleared after 7 days). A failed download gets a note in its place, and the answer still goes through.

Stays the same

  • Answers stay strings: ask_answers_valid and AskUserQuestion's shape don't change.
  • A kit from before this ignores the new attachments field and gets the text only.
  • Outbox cards and the dossier's quick answers: no screenshots.
  • A terminal answer is recorded exactly as it is today.
  • History shows the answer text, plus "📎 N screenshots" when there are any.
  • Who can answer a round, and who reads it, is unchanged. Screenshots follow the same rules.
