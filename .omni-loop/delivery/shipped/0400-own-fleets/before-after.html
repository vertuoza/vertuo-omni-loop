Your own fleets, or none

Today a new workspace gets stuck at the fleet step, and only a database migration can add a fleet. After this PRD, a fleet is optional, a player can play solo, and each workspace's owner raises its own fleets on /app/fleets. Nothing comes from Vertuoza.
