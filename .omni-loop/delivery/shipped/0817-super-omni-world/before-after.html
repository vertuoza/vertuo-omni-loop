PRD 817 · before / after

SUPER OMNI WORLD

An original SNES-style platformer that unlocks at LV 2. It has three stages, your own hero, entropy to stomp, coins and a flag. You can play it from the arcade room, and from the corner Game Boy while Claude works. It is built on Phaser 4 and uses no Nintendo assets.

The game room

Today

One game. Reaching LV 2 opens nothing new.

After

The second cabinet is SUPER OMNI WORLD. It opens at LV 2. Below that it shows REACH LV 2 TO PLAY.

The corner Game Boy, while Claude works

Today

Straight into Entropy Invaders, whatever your level.

After · LV 2 and above

A two-line picker. Below LV 2 the dock goes straight into Invaders, as today. A question from Claude pauses either game.

The game: stage 1-1 on the tall grid (320×288)

Today

Nothing to run and jump in.

After
  • Your own hero (32×48, your colours). B runs, and A jumps higher the longer you hold it.
  • Stomp entropy for 50. A coin is 10. The flag adds 10 per second left.
  • 3 lives. 1-1 grass, 1-2 underground, 1-3 castle, then WORLD CLEAR.
  • Phaser loads only when the game opens. If it fails: GAME DID NOT LOAD · A TO RETRY.
