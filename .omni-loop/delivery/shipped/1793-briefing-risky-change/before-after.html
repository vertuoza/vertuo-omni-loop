PRD 1793 — before and after

PRD 1793 · P1 of the roadmap "Governed knowledge"

An agent can no longer loosen the rules that govern its own work. A change to the briefing (<paths.playbook>/briefing.md, the Never lines every agent reads first) becomes a risky change only a person answers, and the briefing's CODEOWNER is asked to review it.

Functional

Nothing on a screen changes. What changes is what the outbox check says about a range, and the CODEOWNERS block omni kb owners writes.

What stays the same

  • 1726's tiers: a rule is answered by any person through a high item; a locked law needs a CODEOWNER's approving review. The briefing takes the rule tier, not the law tier.
  • Every other playbook form, and the kit's own template of the briefing.
  • Knowledge and enforce PRs are never blocked; invade PRs are not graded.
  • The briefing's text: this PRD does not edit it.
