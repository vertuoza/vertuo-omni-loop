PRD 1762 · OMNI KART · today and after

OMNI KART options and podium

OMNI KART gets an OPTIONS screen kept in the player's browser: the keys that drive the kart, the race's laps, the rivals' difficulty, and the kart's colour and design. The arrows drive and SPACE fires the item without setting anything. Every race that ends at the line now shows a podium before the results.

Stays the same

  • The on-screen pad on a phone, the keys of the menus, and the keys of every other game of the arcade.
  • The board's rule (the lowest three-lap time is kept), submit_score(), the scores' table and the kart grid's access rules. There is no migration.
  • COMET RING, its boxes and its tip-over. The circuit's changes come in their own PRD, and so does the tunnel.
  • The cabinet's still: CLASSIC in FLEET colour is today's kart.
