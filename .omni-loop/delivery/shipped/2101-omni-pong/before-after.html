OMNI PONG · PRD 2101

The arcade's fifth game, unlocked at LV 5: a Pong against an AI, steered by the voice. A sound above the pitch limit moves the paddle up, a sound below moves it down, and silence holds it still. Multiplayer comes in a PRD of its own.

The match. The ball speeds up at every return, to a cap; the AI follows it with a delay and a top speed, so a long rally ends in an AI miss. An AI miss scores a point. The third point conceded ends the match, and the points scored are sent once. A point conceded never adds to the score.
