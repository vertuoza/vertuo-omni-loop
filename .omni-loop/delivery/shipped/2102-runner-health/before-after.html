Runner health: before and after

PRD 2102 · Every omni runner reports its machine's CPU, memory and disk, and the Claude tokens its jobs use, every 15 seconds. The Runners page shows them live, with the last hour's trend. A runner under pressure stops taking new jobs, and a queued job says why it waits. The figures are examples.

Functional

Today

  • The runner claims a job whenever a slot is free, however loaded the machine is.
  • The page shows Online or Busy until the machine stops answering, then Offline, with no reason given.
  • Only ask rounds sum tokens. A job's tokens and the machine's are never counted.

After

  • Sample: CPU %, 1-minute load and cores, memory used of total (MemAvailable on Linux), and disk free on ~/wt.
  • Pause: memory above 85 %, disk free under 10 GB, or load above 1.5 × cores. The runner opens no new next, and its running jobs are untouched. --max-memory, --min-disk and --max-load change the limits.
  • Tokens: per job, from the transcripts of its worktree and every folder below it (sub-agents included). Per machine, over the last 5 h and since midnight UTC.
  • Only numbers leave the machine. A failed report never stops the runner, and the pause works with no server.
