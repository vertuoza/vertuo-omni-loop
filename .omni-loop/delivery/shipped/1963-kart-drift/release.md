title: OMNI KART learns to drift on slippery ice
---
FROST EIGHT's ice now slides, and two of its bends freeze over. SPACE hops into a drift that pays a blue or orange mini-turbo, GAS at the right beat of the countdown gives a rocket start, and a kart tucked behind another is pulled past it. ITEM moves to SHIFT.
