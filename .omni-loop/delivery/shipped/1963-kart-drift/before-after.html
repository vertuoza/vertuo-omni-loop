PRD 1963 · OMNI KART · THE DRIFT

A slippery ice, a hop that drifts into a mini-turbo, a rocket start and the slipstream

Each row shows OMNI KART today beside OMNI KART after this PRD. FROST EIGHT's ice slides for real and gains two icy bends. SPACE hops into a drift that pays a blue or orange turbo. The gas pressed during the 2 pays a rocket start. A kart's slipstream pulls the one behind it. HARD rivals drift too. Snow, marks, sparks and sounds make all of it felt. FROST EIGHT's ice: the lake, then two icy bends

Today · the lake only

The diagonals cross the lake in a straight line: a player barely steers on the ice.

After · the lake, bend 4 and bend 8

Each patch is centred on the middle of its bend's curve and covers the road's width. The track's lake becomes a list of ice discs, the lake first; the minimap shows all three in light blue. The rivals pick their speed as today and slide wide into the powder on an icy bend.

The hop and the drift

Today · steer only

◀ ▶ turn the nose at turnRate: 2.6 rad/s at grip speed, 1.56 at the top speed. There is no hop, no drift and no charge. A BOOST comes from an item or a pad, nothing else.

After · HOP (SPACE) held through a bend
  • The hop: 4 px for 0.25 s, drawn only. The kart takes boxes, pads, BLOBs, ORBs and pushes, tips and falls as on the ground.
  • The drift starts when the hop lands with HOP still held, ◀ or ▶ held, at half the top speed or more.
  • It turns towards its side at 2.2 rad/s with that arrow, 1.5 with none, 0.9 with the other. The nose leads the path by up to 0.45 rad.

Lost, with no turbo: ▼, a BLOB or ORB hit, a fall, a tip-over, the tree line, the speed under half, a ramp's jump, the race's end. On ice the lower grip and the larger slip hold, so a drift on the lake runs wider.

The rocket start

Today · the countdown waits

Nothing moves before GO, and what is held during 3 · 2 · 1 changes nothing. At GO every kart starts from rest the same way.

After · when GAS last went down

GAS already held when the countdown starts counts as pressed during the 3. The rivals start with a turbo at 20 % (EASY), 40 % (NORMAL) and 70 % (HARD), drawn from the race's seed, and never stall. In MULTI each player times their own.

The slipstream

Today · no slipstream

A kart right behind another goes no faster for it. The only contact between karts is the push that keeps them apart.

After · 1.2 s in the cone gives a BOOST of 0.6 s

Leaving the cone starts the count again from 0. Every kart gives and takes one: the player, the rivals, and in MULTI the other players' karts at their carried positions. A kart in the air or falling neither gives nor takes.
