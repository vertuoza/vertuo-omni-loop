`main` through one draft feature PR (`Closes #2809`); each slice is a sub-PR from
`feat/unlosable-jobs--<slice>` into the feature branch (`Part of #2809`).

## Per slice: done when

### s1 (deep: a migration and the loss functions run in one transaction)

- `runner_jobs` has `resumes` (unique, references `runner_jobs`), `attempt` (1 to 3, default 1) and
  `resume_after`; old rows read `attempt 1`, no `resumes`.
- `runners_mark_lost`, the re-registration loss and the unstarted-claim loss each queue, in the same
  transaction, one resume for a job whose skill is in `RESUMABLE_SKILLS` and whose attempt is under
  3, with the same line, repository, sender (key and maker included), target, `attempt + 1` and
  `resume_after` 1 minute (attempt 2) or 5 minutes (attempt 3) later; a second mark of the same job
  queues nothing.
- A cancel, a refusal, the skill's own failure, a timeout and a skill outside `RESUMABLE_SKILLS`
  queue nothing.
- `runner_claim()` hands out no job before its `resume_after`.
- `RESUMABLE_SKILLS` (`yolo`, `yolo-fix`, `wave`, `do-work`, `pr-care`, `bug-fix`) lives in
  `kit/lib/runner/line.ts`, and a test holds it to a subset of `RUNNABLE_SKILLS`.
- `supabase/checks/runner_resume.sql` proves each line above and runs as its own step of the
  `supabase` workflow; `supabase/database.types.ts` is regenerated; the service tests read the new
  columns.

### s2 (deep: git plumbing that must never move a branch or lose a file)

- Before `cleanUp` and before the sweep removes a stranded folder, the runner lists the checkout's
  worktrees inside the job's folder and, for each, its uncommitted changes (tracked, and new files
  git does not ignore) and its commits not on `origin`.
- Nothing found: nothing is written, and the folder is removed as today.
- Otherwise a snapshot commit is made through a temporary index (no branch moves, the worktree is
  unchanged), and one bundle holds `omni-checkpoint/<branch>` for each branch, with only the commits
  `origin` lacks.
- A file git ignores (a `.env`) is never in the bundle.
- Until s4 sends it, the bundle is a local save: the folder is kept, under `--keep-budget`
  (5 GB by default); over it, the oldest kept folder is removed and its loss recorded in a manifest
  file beside it; a kept folder goes at its `keep_until` (7 days).
- Tested on temporary git repositories with a nested worktree.

### s3

- `/api/runners/jobs/<id>/github-token` refuses a job that has ended, whatever its status.
- A refused report (409) stops that job's session at once, and its cleanup (s2's save first) runs.
- Service and runner tests prove both.

### s4 (deep: customer code leaves the runner)

- `runner_checkpoints` (job, runner, kind `portable` or `local`, manifest, bytes, storage path,
  `keep_until`, `deleted_at`) and the private bucket `runner-checkpoints` (100 MB per object)
  exist; only the workspace's members read their rows, and writes go through security-definer
  functions; `supabase/checks/runner_checkpoints.sql` proves a non-member reads nothing and runs as
  its own workflow step.
- `POST /api/runners/jobs/<id>/checkpoint` gives the job's runner a signed upload link of minutes,
  then records the manifest, read by `CheckpointManifestSchema` on both sides.
- A failed or refused upload records the save as `local`, with its runner, size and `keep_until`.
- The handoff note travels masked and cut to 8 KB; no credential reaches a manifest.

### s5 (deep: the claim's routing and a restore that must not clobber GitHub's branch)

- The claim of a job with `resumes` carries its chain's saves: portable ones with a signed download
  link of minutes and their manifest, local ones with their manifest.
- The runner fetches each bundle into the checkout as `omni-checkpoint/<branch>` and writes
  `~/logs/job-<id>.resume.md`: the attempt (`2 of 3`), each branch and whether it fast-forwards from
  `origin`'s or diverged, the handoff note, what stayed behind, and the rule to take a branch's
  checkpoint (`git merge --ff-only omni-checkpoint/<branch>`, or a merge when diverged) before
  anything else; the session's prompt points at it.
- A slice claimed by a job is held by its chain: the resume may build it, no other job may.
- A chain holding a local save is claimed by that save's runner while it is online at
  `resume_after`; otherwise by any runner, whose note names what stayed behind.

### s6

- The `checkpoints-forget` kind runs every hour on the App's job ledger, deletes from the bucket
  the bundles of chains that ended (a job of them done, cancelled or not resumed) and any bundle 7
  days old, and stamps `deleted_at`.
- Tested on the ledger's fake store with a fixed clock.

### s7 (`ui: yes`)

- A lost job reads **Lost · resuming** in `--ask-red`, `<runner> went silent at <time>` under it,
  linking its resume.
- A resume reads `after a loss · job <id>`, then `attempt <k> of 3`, and once started one of
  `Resumed from the <time> save · <n> branches`, `Started over: nothing was unsaved`,
  `<n> files stayed on <runner>`.
- **Lost 3 times** in `--ask-red`, the losses' times under it, Resume its one action.
- A runner keeping a local save reads `Keeps <size> until <day time>` under its name.
- The `runners` draft screen's mockup shows these rows; the word pass finds nothing.

### s8

- `kit/lib/runner/resume.e2e.test.ts` runs two in-process runners against an in-memory App: box-1
  is killed mid-slice with uncommitted edits and an unpushed commit in a nested worktree; box-3
  claims the resume only after its wait, and its worktree holds both; box-1's late report and token
  refresh are refused; a third loss stops the chain and queues nothing.
- No model call: the session is a fake that edits files.
