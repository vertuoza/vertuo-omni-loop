PRD 2809: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

A runner goes silent or restarts. Its job turns red, Failed · runner lost. The work its slice had not pushed is deleted with the folder. Nothing starts again until a person notices and presses Resume, which starts from scratch.

After

The lost job reads Lost · resuming. A minute later another runner takes it up as attempt 2 of 3, from the save the lost runner left: its unpushed commits and edits come back. A runner that keeps a save it could not upload says so on its row, with its size and until when. After three losses the job stops and lists them, and Resume is a person's again.

omni-app

Functional

Today

The App runs no job about runners.

After

The App deletes a chain's saved bundles once the chain ends, and any bundle 7 days old, every hour.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the App has no screen

supabase

Functional

Today

A lost job is a dead end in the database.

After

The database queues the resume in the same moment it records the loss, once, and holds it back until its wait ends.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the database has no screen

kit

Functional

Today

omni runner removes a finished job's folder, nested worktrees included, with nothing saved.

After

omni runner saves what GitHub does not hold before it removes anything, uploads it, or keeps it under a disk budget, and restores a lost job's save when it takes up its resume.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the CLI prints lines, unchanged but for one per save

Flow

Sequence (source)
box-1 (omni runner) ─ running job 41 ─▶ silent at 02:14
galaxy ─ runners_mark_lost ─▶ supabase: job 41 failed · runner lost; insert job 7f3a (resumes 41, attempt 2, resume_after 02:15)
box-1 back ─ report job 41 ─▶ galaxy: 409 refused ─▶ box-1 stops the session
box-1 ─ checkpoint: bundle of unpushed work ─▶ galaxy POST /api/runners/jobs/41/checkpoint ─▶ signed upload ─▶ Supabase Storage runner-checkpoints
box-1 ─ manifest (Zod) ─▶ galaxy ─▶ supabase runner_checkpoints
box-3 ─ next (after 02:15) ─▶ runner_claim(): job 7f3a + checkpoints with signed download links
box-3 ─ fetch bundle ─▶ omni-checkpoint/<branch> ─ resume note ─▶ session merges the checkpoint, carries on
omni-app hourly checkpoints-forget ─▶ storage: delete bundles of ended chains and older than 7 days

Drawn as text: the flow has no Mermaid diagram to render.

Deploy order

  1. The migration (columns, table, bucket, runner_claim() and the loss functions). Old runners and the old app keep working: the new columns have defaults.
  2. The arcade (API route, service, Runners page) and the App's forget job, together through the release commit.
  3. The kit (omni runner): runners save and restore once updated; an older runner still gets its jobs resumed, without a save.
