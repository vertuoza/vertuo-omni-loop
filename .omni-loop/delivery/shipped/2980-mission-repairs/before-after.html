PRD 2980: before and after

omni-app

Functional

Today

A mission stops a PRD at its first failure, or after a second hold, and says only “Take it to ready by hand”. Nothing looks at why.

After

A mission sends a repair first: it finds the cause, fixes it on the mission branch and runs the step again. One cause gets 3 repairs, a mission 9; then the PRD stops and explains what was tried and what would fix it. An Omni Loop bug is reported once, with none of the workspace’s work in it.

galaxy

Functional

Today

The Operation room says “PRD 2801 stopped: its job failed. Take it to ready by hand.”

After

The room says “Repairing (1 of 3) · 8 left in this mission” with the cause and the fix PR, and, when it stops, the cause, the 3 repairs tried with their PRs, the proposed fix and Resume. A PRD that waits on an Omni Loop bug links it.

kit

Functional

Today

No skill looks at a failed step. A bug fix always starts from the default branch.

After

A new skill, /omni:repair, runs on a runner: it writes a retro of the failure, names its cause with a fingerprint, and starts a bug fix into the mission branch, or writes a scrubbed report when Omni Loop itself is wrong.

database

Functional

Today

Nothing counts how often a mission tried to fix a cause.

After

Every repair is a row, and the database itself refuses a 4th repair of one cause and a 10th in a mission, so a mission can never loop.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. The migration 20270125140000_mission_repairs.sql (two tables, four event kinds): nothing writes them yet.
  2. The kit (/omni:repair, /omni:bug-fix --base, kit/lib/repair/), released before the App sends a repair job.
  3. The Omni App (repairs in the supervisor, the landing of a fix PR, the report filer) and Galaxy (the two room states), together.

Inert until a mission’s job fails. Rolled back by a repair budget of 0: a failure stops its PRD as today.
