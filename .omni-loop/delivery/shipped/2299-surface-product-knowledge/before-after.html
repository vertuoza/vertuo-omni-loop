PRD 2299: before and after

kit

Functional

When an agent session starts. Today it learns the product's constituents and nothing else of its knowledge. After, a short block follows them: the product's words (and the ones it avoids), its Legal entries, and who it is for.

Today
Constituents of omni-loop (synced just now): …Statement: Omni Loop turns a written product idea …Never:  never#1  Never ships without a person choosing to …  …# Briefing …
After (at most 3 KB)
Constituents of omni-loop (synced just now): …Statement: Omni Loop turns a written product idea …Never: …Knowledge of omni-loop (synced just now): words to use, laws to respect, people to build for.Words: Workspace (not: team) · Runner (not: bot, worker) · Skill · Fleet · …       → meanings: omni kb glossaryLegal: L-GDPR-1 Only members of a workspace may read … · L-AIACT-1 … · …       → sources: omni kb legalPersonas: F-E Developer (skeptical) · B-E DEv (skeptical) · Paul - Product Manager (excited) · …       → who they are: omni business show# Briefing …

When a skill writes a spec, a plan, a slice or a fix. Today the words come from a glossary file most repositories do not have. After, brainstorm, plan, do-work and bug-fix read the product's glossary from the Omni page; brainstorm and do-work read its Legal entries where the work touches personal data, keys, logs or licences, and brainstorm names them in the spec's Risks.

When someone writes the wrong word. Today nothing notices. After, a spec in the inbox that says an avoided word gets a warning naming the word to use, and the check stays green; a mockup gets the same finding in the word pass.

Today
$ omni check inboxinbox: ok
After
$ omni check inbox2299-…/spec.md: "team" ×3 → say Workspace   (warning)inbox: ok

Offline: the block is the last copy, with its age; with nothing at all, one line, and the session carries on.
