PRD 1808 · Today beside after. Measured signed in on production, 2026-10-13.

Functional

What one visit to the game asks of the server.

Today
  1. Open /play: the server reads the workspace, the galaxy, fleets, you, crew, owner, XP, scores…
  2. …and the dossier of every planet: 215 × 2 = 430 database calls, fired at once.
  3. …and parses the star chart.
  4. Nothing reaches the browser for 7.2 s, then 2.6 MB of HTML: every planet's whole log (1.17 MB), every dossier, the chart.
  5. Open a planet: everything is already there.
After
  1. Open /play: the server reads what the map needs: the workspace, the galaxy (cached), fleets, you, crew, owner, XP, scores. ~10 calls.
  2. Planets go without their logs; no dossier, no star chart.
  3. The map is playable in ≤ 1.5 s, from ≤ 1 MB of HTML.
  4. Open a planet: GET /api/arcade/planets/<key> gives its dossier and its last 8 log lines, ~3 calls, kept for the session.
  5. Open the star chart: GET /api/arcade/chart, once per session.
