Five more fleet mascots

A workspace that raises its own fleets picks each fleet's mascot from a library drawn for Vertuoza's six. After this PRD the library has eleven: Atom Eve, a shark, a turtle, Allen the Alien and a robot join the beaver, the octopod, Picsou, the agent, the pirate and Invincible.

2 · The new cast, both frames

These are drafts, forged with the repository's own forge. Each card shows frame 1, frame 2, and the two frames alternating. The drawn sprites may refine the pixels but keep each look.

Atom Eve atom-eve

Pink bodysuit with magenta trim, long red hair, a light pink atom on the chest, one hand raised holding pink energy.

Frame 2

Shark shark

Upright and blue-grey, with a white belly, a dorsal fin, fins for arms, a toothy grin, and the four stripes on a collar with a navy tie.

Frame 2

Turtle turtle

A green turtle with an olive hex-patterned shell, its head out front, and a bandana in the four stripes.

Frame 2

Allen the Alien allen

A wide, round pale-blue head with one big green-irised eye, and a green suit with white chest bands.

Robot robot

A boxy retro steel robot: an antenna with a red light, cyan eyes behind a dark visor, a mouth grille, and the four stripes on a chest panel.

Frame 2
