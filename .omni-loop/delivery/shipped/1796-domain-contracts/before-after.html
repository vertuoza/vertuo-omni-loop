PRD 1796 — before and after

PRD 1796 · P4 of the roadmap "Governed knowledge"

Functional

A fourth kind of register entry, the domain contract: one plain line on an entity's structure or lifecycle, and a pointer to the file that enforces it.

1. The register folder

Today

knowledge/
  product/
    principles.md   P-PRODUCT-n
    rules.md        BR-PRODUCT-n
    invariants.md   N-PRODUCT-n
  domains/invoicing/
    README.md
    principles.md · rules.md · invariants.md
  contracts.md  ← never read
  • Three kinds, closed: EntryKind, LAYER_FILES, ID_SOURCE.
  • A ## DC-… heading is skipped as an unknown heading.
  • How an entity moves through its states lives only in the code.

After

knowledge/
  product/
    principles.md · rules.md · invariants.md
    contracts.md    DC-PRODUCT-n   (optional)
  domains/invoicing/
    README.md
    principles.md · rules.md · invariants.md
    contracts.md    DC-INVOICING-n (optional)
  • Four kinds: principle, rule, invariant, contract.
  • A folder without contracts.md stays green.
  • Not in cross-domain/: a DC- id there is refused.

## DC-INVOICING-1

An invoice moves Draft → Sent → Partially paid → Paid,
or is offset by a credit note. A project has many invoices.

Points to: src/invoicing/invoice-status.ts, src/invoicing/invoice.schema.ts
Source: PRD 412
Stated: 2026-10-13

A pointer, never a second copy: no field list. No Enforced by:, Locked:, Proposed law: or Governs:.
