PRD 3129: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today
  • A dropdown opens the operating system's own picker: a grey list in the system font, light even in the dark theme, with no mark of the product.
  • Each page draws its closed box its own way: Ask's category chip, the share picker, the dossier's version picker and the products forms all differ.
  • Nothing stops a new page from adding one more raw dropdown.
After
  • Every dropdown on the app pages opens the product's own list: its colours in every theme, its type and spacing, a check beside the option picked.
  • It works by mouse, by touch and by keyboard: the arrows move, typing a letter jumps, Enter picks, Escape or a click outside closes it.
  • Two sizes: a full one in forms, a small one in chips, toolbars and sorts. On a phone the list stays inside the screen and every option is big enough to tap.
  • Each screen still saves exactly what was picked.
  • A new raw dropdown fails the design check before it can merge.

Flow

Deploy order

One deployment of the galaxy app (Vercel), after the feature PR merges. No migration, no API, no other service. The ratchet rule runs in CI from the same merge.
