PRD 1633 · before / after

OMNI DUET, the fourth cabinet

A player who reaches LV 4 gets a new game: two orbs circle the Omni Loop crest, ◀ ▶ turn them, and they thread through waves of falling Entropy blocks. Four chapters, then endless mode worth double. Three lives, and a life lost never earns a point.

The game room, wide grid

Today

Three cabinets, all shown at once. At LV 4 nothing new opens.

After

Four cabinets. The row keeps three in view and scrolls by one so the chosen cabinet is always shown: 1–3 with the cursor on 1 to 3, 2–4 with it on 4. The tall grid still shows one cabinet a page. Below LV 4 the fourth cabinet says REACH LV 4 TO PLAY.

Playing a chapter

Today
no dodge game in the arcade

One shooter, one platformer, one racer.

After · tall grid, 320×288

The orbs stay opposite on one circle; ◀ and ▶ held turn them at a constant speed. A wave is passed when its last block falls below the circle untouched. The wide grid draws the same 320×288 playfield centred, so a run plays the same on a phone and a computer.

A life lost

Today, in SUPER OMNI WORLD

The route that let a player farm points by dying twice in 1-3. OMNI DUET must not repeat it.

After, in OMNI DUET

A hit costs a life and restarts the chapter from its first wave, with the score back to what it was when the chapter began (here 200, after chapters I and II). Play waits for START. No sequence of hits ends higher than the same run without them, and a test proves it on every wave.
