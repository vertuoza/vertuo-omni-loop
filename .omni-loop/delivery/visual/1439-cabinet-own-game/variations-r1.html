Visual fix #1439 · each cabinet shows its own game

The game room, as a player at LV 4 sees it with the three cabinets lit. Today every lit cabinet's screen shows the Invaders formation. ENTROPY INVADERS keeps it in every option below. Only the OMNI KART and SUPER OMNI WORLD screens change. Locked and SOON cabinets keep their dark screen and lock.

Every sprite is the real one from @omni/design. The Mode 7 floor in A is rendered by the game's own renderFloor over the real COMET RING texture. The pixel font isn't loaded here, so the text falls back to a monospace face.
