## The question, in plain words

The app ships a prebuilt copy of its code that a test keeps up to date, and the plan did not list that copy as ground this slice may touch. Is it fine that the slice rebuilt it?

## The decision, in plain words

The slice rebuilt the app's prebuilt copy with the repository's own build command and committed it, so the test that guards it stays green.

## The intro, for fun

The plan drew a fence around the slice, and the app's prebuilt copy sat just outside it.

## The punchline, for fun

We stepped over to dust it off, and touched nothing else on the way back.

## The options, in plain words

A. A. Rebuild the copy in the slice that changes the app, as done.
B. B. Leave it stale and let a later step rebuild it, with the guarding test red until then.
C. C. Name the copy in the plan's ground for every slice that touches the app.

## What I had to decide

Whether a slice that changes the app may always rebuild its prebuilt copy, or whether plans should name that copy as part of the ground.

## What I did meanwhile

The rebuilt copy is committed on the slice branch; it is generated, never edited by hand.

## What it costs to change later

Nothing to undo: the copy is regenerated by one command on any later change.
