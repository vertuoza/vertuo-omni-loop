## The question, in plain words

Each obstacle now scores on the first moment all of it is below the circle, and only then, even when a spinning rod's tip would swing back above the line, while the wave is still passed at the same moment as before. Is that the rule you want?

## The decision, in plain words

An obstacle scores once, the first moment every part of it is below the lowest point the orbs reach; a gate counts as one. The wave is passed when its last obstacle has scored, which is exactly when it was passed before, so the waves themselves, endless mode included, play as they did.

## The intro, for fun

The rod spun past the orbs and, for a moment, waved its tip back up at them.

## The punchline, for fun

Too late: the point was already on the board.

## The options, in plain words

A. Score each obstacle the first moment all of it is below the circle, once, and keep it until a hit (built).
B. Score a spinning rod only once the whole disc it sweeps is below the circle: a little later than its blocks, the wave still passed at the same moment.
C. Score every obstacle of a wave only when the wave is passed, 1 each (2 in endless mode): the points arrive together, as before, on the new scale.

## What I had to decide

Whether an obstacle's point is given the first moment it is wholly below the circle and kept until a hit, as built.

## What I did meanwhile

The arcade scores each obstacle once, at that moment; a hit gives back every point of the chapter, or of the endless wave.

## What it costs to change later

A different moment is one line in the waves' count and the tests that read it: no stored data changes.
