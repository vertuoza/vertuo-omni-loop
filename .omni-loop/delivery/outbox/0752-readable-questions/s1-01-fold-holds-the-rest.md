## The question, in plain words

When a long question is folded, should the fold repeat the whole question, or only what the bold lead above it leaves out? And what should its word count count?

## The decision, in plain words

The fold shows only what the lead leaves out, so nothing is read twice, and its word count is the length of the whole question.

## The intro, for fun

A long question walks into a fold and asks how much of itself to bring.

## The punchline, for fun

It packed only what the headline did not already say.

## The options, in plain words

A. Fold only the rest and count the whole question: nothing is read twice, and the count says how long the question really is.
B. Fold the whole question and count it: the fold repeats the lead, so it reads as the full question its title promises.
C. Fold only the rest and count only the rest: the count describes exactly what opening the fold shows.

## What I had to decide

Whether the fold holds the rest of the question or the whole of it, and which text its word count measures.

## What I did meanwhile

The page shows the lead in bold and folds only the rest; the summary counts the words of the whole question, as the drawn page did.

## What it costs to change later

A few lines in the long-question module: the rest is built from the sentences the lead did not take.
