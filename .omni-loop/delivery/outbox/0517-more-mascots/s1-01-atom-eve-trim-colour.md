## The question, in plain words

Should Atom Eve's gloves, boots, belt and collar be a dark magenta pink, or the purple the first drafts used?

## The decision, in plain words

We drew them in a dark magenta, the deepest shade of her own pink, because the design asks for magenta trim and the purple read as violet.

## The intro, for fun

Atom Eve got dressed, and the trim had a quiet identity crisis between magenta and purple.

## The punchline, for fun

Magenta won by a shade, and the purple went back in the paint box for now.

## The options, in plain words

A. Dark magenta from her own pink, the option built.
B. Plasma purple, as the first drafts of the design show.
C. Dark magenta trim, with plasma purple sparks around the energy ball.

## What I had to decide

Which colour Atom Eve's trim (gloves, boots, belt, collar) is drawn in.

## What I did meanwhile

The pink material's darkest tone, a dark magenta; no plasma purple on her at all.
