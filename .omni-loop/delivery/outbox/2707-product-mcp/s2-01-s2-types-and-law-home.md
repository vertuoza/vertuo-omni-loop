## The question, in plain words

The plan gave this part only the database change and its check, but the team's rule says the generated list of database shapes is refreshed with every database change, and the plan did not say what the law home answer looks like. What should this part do about both?

## The decision, in plain words

It refreshed the generated list of database shapes with the change, so the next parts can use the new switch and the build stays green. It answers the law home as the repository's name with its newest saved knowledge, read whatever repositories a key is limited to.

## The intro, for fun

A plan that forgot the map still needs the map.

## The punchline, for fun

So the map was redrawn, with nothing new on it but the new road.

## The options, in plain words

A. A. This part refreshes the generated list of database shapes, and answers the law home with its newest saved knowledge.
B. B. The agent part refreshes that list later, and the law home answers only the repository's name.
C. C. The wave refreshes that list after merging, and the law home answers as in A.

## What I had to decide

Adopt A, unless the generated types belong to another slice or the law home should carry only the repository's name.

## What I did meanwhile

Slices s3 and s4 build on the refreshed types and read the law home's pack from its pack id.

## What it costs to change later

Undoing it is regenerating one file and changing one field of one function: no data moves.
