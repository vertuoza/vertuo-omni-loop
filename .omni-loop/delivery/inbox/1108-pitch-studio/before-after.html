PRD #1108 · before / after

Pitch studio: a launch video, not a slide and a screen recording

Today a pitch is a still slide, a scrolled recording and an end card. After, it is an animated storyboard in the product's own look, previewed in a studio, with every outside tool swappable. Everything stays free.
