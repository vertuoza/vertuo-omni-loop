Your context and your PRD, always in view

Today the bottom of Claude Code shows keyboard hints. To know how full the context is, or where the PRD you are building stands, you ask. After, every session opened in a repository with the loop shows two lines there: the model, the context bar, your 5-hour usage and ask mode; then the PRD this session works on, its stage, the wave and slices while it is built, and its open outbox items. omni init switches it on for the whole repository.

omni init: switched on for everyone

One key in the repository's committed .claude/settings.json. A status line that is not the kit's is never overwritten; a person who prefers their own keeps it in .claude/settings.local.json.

  wrote   .omni-loop/config.yml

The status line is on for everyone who opens

To remove the loop: delete .omni-loop/ and the

commit. The labels and the App installation
stay.

Someone else's status line prints kept … (its statusLine is not the kit's), even with --force. A settings.json that is not valid JSON is left untouched.

HOME: Easy in, easy out

The one-folder promise is no longer exact, so HOME says what omni init now writes and what to delete to stop.

today

GET IN

  1. omni init adds one folder to your repository: .omni-loop/.
  2. …

GET OUT

  • Delete .omni-loop/ and commit. That's it.
after

GET IN

  1. omni init adds one folder to your repository, .omni-loop/, and a status line to .claude/settings.json.
  2. …

GET OUT

  • Delete .omni-loop/ and the statusLine in .claude/settings.json, and commit. That's it.
