PRD 3375: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

A business law on the Omni page shows its statement, its Why, its domains and its owner. Nothing says that a rollout group or new tenants get the feature differently, nor whether anyone checked.

A Legal entry's page shows the entry and who owns it, nothing about rollouts that touch it.

After

Each law's row has a Differs for column: who gets it differently (Internal, New tenants), the feature, the flag behind it, and how and when it was checked, marked stale after 30 days (or the file's own limit).

A Legal entry a variant names shows the same Differs for block.

A new Law variants section lines every condition up against every audience of the product, with what happens when it is on and off.

kit

Functional

Today

omni kb business-laws and an agent's get_knowledge print a law's statement and Why. Vertuoza keeps who gets what in a file of its own, outside the knowledge folder, that no reader sees.

After

A product writes product/law-variants.json in its law home's knowledge folder. omni check knowledge refuses what is wrong in it, by path. omni kb business-laws and get_knowledge print a Differs for line under each law; search_knowledge finds a law by its flag.

omni kb law-variants prints the file in words, and --base <ref> prints only what changed (BETA: off → on), so a generated file is reviewed as a list of changes.

Flow

To settle — diagram not rendered: omni before-after render hung past its limit here.

  1. The law home holds knowledge/product/law-variants.json; omni check knowledge grades it.
  2. A merge to its default branch: the Omni App keeps it in the knowledge pack (Supabase, knowledge_packs and their files).
  3. The Omni page reads the pack as the member (row-level security): Business laws, a Legal entry, Law variants.
  4. The knowledge MCP reads the law home pack: get_knowledge, search_knowledge.
  5. The terminal reads this checkout, or the pack through omni kb pack get: omni kb business-laws, omni kb law-variants.

Deploy order

  1. One merge ships both: the kit (kit/dist/omni.mjs, the plugin) and the galaxy app on its next production deploy. No migration, no order between them: a page without the file, or a kit without the command, simply shows no variant.
  2. Then each product adds its file (Vertuoza: nimbus-automation-plan, tracked on team-platformengineering#1092).
