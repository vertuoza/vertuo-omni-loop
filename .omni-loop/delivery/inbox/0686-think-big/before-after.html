PRD 686 · before / after

Go wide before
you go narrow.

Today every idea goes straight into /omni:brainstorm, which is built to converge. /omni:think-big adds a studio in front of it: role agents argue over rendered concepts, round after round, while a product or design person reacts and picks. A vast idea leaves it as a concept in the inbox, and each of its areas then becomes a PRD.

The loop, today beside after

A vast idea: "one agenda for every employee"

Today
  1. One line into /omni:brainstorm.
  2. It asks about purpose and cuts every feature the brief does not need.
  3. The idea holds several subsystems, so it is flagged as too big and split into PRDs.
  4. The first PRD is designed. Nobody has seen what the whole could be.

Nothing goes wide first, nothing is rendered to react to, and nobody argues for the bold direction before the scope is cut.

After
  1. Gate: it is vast, of the product kind. A tweak would get /omni:visual-fix; a feature, the choice of /omni:brainstorm or a lite run.
  2. Fuel: today's screens, tokens and knowledge, plus references from other products and industries.
  3. Round 1: six to eight storyboard cards, each with its one wow moment. The person reacts.
  4. Rounds 2 and on: the studio remixes the survivors into clickable prototypes.
  5. Crown: the person picks; a vision tour and an area map, wedge first.
  6. Inbox: a docs-only concept PR a person merges, then one /omni:brainstorm --concept per area.

The whole is seen, argued over and picked before it is cut into PRDs, and each PRD starts from it.

Before any concept · the gate

Is it really big?

The scale and the kind are said out loud, and the person can override them. In doubt between feature and vast, it takes vast.

Tweak "make the sidebar darker" → the /omni:visual-fix line; nothing is written
Feature "export an invoice as PDF" → the person picks: /omni:brainstorm now, or a lite run ending in a one-area concept
Vast "a brand new identity" · "rewrite it in TypeScript" · "an agenda for every employee" → the studio runs; the boards adapt to the kind
kindthe artists' lenseswhat the boards showuser personas
product10×, steal from another industry, delight, contrarianstoryboard cards, then clickable prototypesthe brief's end users
identityevolution, revolution, heritage, unexpectedbrand boards (type, colour, motion, voice) applied to the real screenscustomers, and staff who see it daily
platformclean slate, strangler, tooling first, contrariantarget architecture, migration paths, a demo of what it unlocksdevelopers and operators, and the end user who feels it
