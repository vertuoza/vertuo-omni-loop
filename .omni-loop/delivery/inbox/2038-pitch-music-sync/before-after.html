PRD 2038 · /omni:pitch

Pitch music that lands on a measured rise

The same 30-second storyboard, its sync point 2 s into the before/after scene. Today the track's 8th second is assumed to be its key moment. After, the track is measured and its rise lands on the reveal.

Where the music lands

Today

The track starts 2 s into itself so that its 8th second meets the sync point. The track is still quiet there; its rise arrives 6 s later, in the middle of another scene.

After

The track is measured: its rise is at 14 s. It starts 8 s into itself so that the rise meets the sync point, and lasts past the video's end. Lengthen a scene before it and the start is recomputed.

Choosing the track

Today 
$ omni pitch render <dir>
(the mood's shortest track that covers
 the video is downloaded and used;
 nobody hears it before the render)

The person learns which track was used from the credit in pitch.json, after the render.

After 
$ omni pitch music <dir> --list
1. Advertime     · 2:14 · https://archive.org/download/freepd/upbeat/Advertime.mp3
2. Inspiration   · 2:18 · https://archive.org/download/freepd/upbeat/Inspiration.mp3
3. Funshine      · 2:45 · https://archive.org/download/freepd/upbeat/Funshine.mp3
4. City Sunshine · 3:05 · https://archive.org/download/freepd/upbeat/City%20Sunshine.mp3

Skill: Listen to these and pick one by its number,
       or name another mood.
Person: 3

$ omni pitch music <dir> --track "Funshine"
Funshine · 2:45 · music.mp3, music.json
rises: 14.00 s (+7.1 dB), 52.35 s (+4.0 dB)
falls: 40.10 s (-6.2 dB)

Figures above are illustrative. The skill then takes the strongest rise that covers the video and writes "sync": { "scene": "wipe", "at": 2, "cue": 14 }.

What the check says

Today 
$ omni pitch check <dir>
0 errors, 0 warnings
(a sync that starts the video before the
 track, or a track that ends before the
 video, is not seen; "scene": 1 follows
 whatever scene is now second)
After 
$ omni pitch check <dir>
error: meta.music.sync: the video would start 3.0 s
  before the track begins: pick a later rise or a
  later scene
error: meta.music.sync.scene: no scene has the id "wype"
warning: meta.music: the track ends 4.2 s before the video
warning: meta.music.sync.cue: 20 s is not a measured rise
warning: meta.music.sync.scene: name the scene by its id

What stays the same

  • A product whose Pitch settings ask for no music (the default): no question, no download, no measure.
  • A storyboard written before this PRD passes the check with the same findings and renders.
  • The music sources: FreePD's pinned CC0 tracks and the product's own uploaded file.
  • Loudness normalisation, fades, and silence with one line when a track cannot be had.
