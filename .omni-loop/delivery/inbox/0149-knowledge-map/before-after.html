PRD #149 · before / after

Each knowledge domain becomes a star system

Today the knowledge base is 58 entries in three Markdown files, read one at a time. After this PRD the kit builds it into a graph, and the web app draws it twice: a star chart in the arcade, where every entry is a world on its orbit, and a plain map at /knowledge for reading.

Every figure on this page is this repository's real knowledge base on 2026-09-26: 24 principles, 26 rules, 8 invariants, 1 law and 57 proposed. The mockups are drawn to the arcade's grids (640×360 and 320×288).

1 · the graph, for agents and for both maps

From registers to one graph

The registers stay exactly as they are. omni kb graph reads them through the kit's one parser and returns domains, entries and links; --json is the shape both maps and any coding agent read.

2 · the arcade

A STAR CHART in the menu

The menu gains one item after GALAXY MAP. It opens the chart of systems, one sun per domain.

Law · terraformed Proposed · barren Cursor Serves link

◀▶ walk an orbit, ▲▼ step to the nearest world on the next orbit in or out, a tap selects. A opens the reading card with the whole entry: statement, why, what it serves, what serves it, and the PRD it came from. B closes it, then returns to the chart.

3 · the plain page

/knowledge, a map made for reading

The same graph on the /ask pages' reading surface, light and dark. Each entry can be linked to: /knowledge?domain=product&entry=BR-PRODUCT-3.
