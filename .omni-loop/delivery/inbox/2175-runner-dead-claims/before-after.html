Dead job claims: before and after

PRD 2175. When a runner job dies mid-wave, a watcher in omni runner releases the slices the job held and raises the alarm. The next run reuses a released slice when its plan and territory still match, after re-checking it, or closes it and rebuilds it. A runner job never waits on a question. The figures are those of job a8a6bf82 (PRD 1904).

Functional

Today

  1. A job runs /omni:yolo 1904 and claims s1 and s2: draft sub-PRs #1908 and #1909, labelled omni:in-progress.
  2. The job dies after about 450 lines on each, because of Claude's ten-minute stop or a usage limit.
  3. Nothing touches #1908 or #1909. Their branches carry commits beyond the claim, so the board reads them in-flight forever.
  4. The next /omni:yolo 1904 leaves "another job's" slices alone and ends held on #1909.
  5. A person finds out, then marks each sub-PR ready or closes it by hand.
  6. On a red gate with answers.enabled, step 6 asks a question that nobody is there to answer.

After

  1. The job gets OMNI_JOB, and each claim's status comment says job: a8a6bf82 · runner vertuo-agents.
  2. The job dies. The runner's watcher finds the claims that name it and swaps omni:in-progress for omni:released. It comments on each one, and the job row shows Released #1908, #1909.
  3. The board reads a released draft as claimed-stale: takeable at once. A claim nobody saw die becomes takeable after 240 quiet minutes.
  4. omni claim check 1904 s2 runs:
    → reuse: same plan row and every change inside the territory. The run takes the slice over, re-runs the preflight and reviews the diff, then finishes the slice.
    → rebuild: outside territory: …: the run closes the sub-PR with that reason, deletes its branch, and claims the slice afresh in the same run.
  5. Under OMNI_UNATTENDED, nothing asks. A red gate posts the outbox and ends red, and omni ask answers unattended.
