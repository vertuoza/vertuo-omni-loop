Jev decisions

PRD 812. A workspace owner opts in to Jev (TypeSafe AI) with an API key, then hands it three decisions one at a time: the question category, the outbox item risk and the bug-fix risk. Each decision is Off, Shadow or On. Nothing changes for a workspace that never opts in.

1 · Settings

The owner turns Jev on and pastes the key. It is tested with one call and never shown again. Each decision has its own mode, tuning and record.

2 · How a decision is made

Jev is only ever called by the app. Claude sessions ask through omni decide. Every failure means today's answer counts, so Jev can never block a wave or a fix.

What stays the same

  • Every workspace starts with Jev off. Without a key, every page, skill and category works as today.
  • The kit still computes the rank. Needs-a-person items, named-law stops, principle conflicts and the law floor are never Jev's call.
  • Kind, domain and regression in a bug triage stay the agent's.
  • A member can still change a question's category with a click.
  • OpenRouter keeps every other use: the retro, the harvest and the rival suggestions.
