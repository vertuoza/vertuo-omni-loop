PRD 1427 · before / after

OMNI KART races on a space circuit

COMET RING keeps its layout, its rules and its score. It gets the look of a console racer in space, a chiptune race tune, an engine, and a sound for every item, box, hit and wall. Everything is drawn and played in code: no image and no audio file.

On the track

Today

A flat texture: grey road, green grass, plain purple walls. Nothing stands beside the track. The box is a yellow square with a ?, and the HUD is one line of text.

After

Dark metal with neon edges, cyan outside and magenta inside, and neon kerbs. Panel seams on the road. A lunar verge that still halves the top speed. Chevrons before the corner point the way it turns. Pylons and beacons line the track, an arch spans the start line, and asteroids, a satellite and a wreck stand off the road. The box is a glowing cube. Every kart casts a shadow, and an ORB hit bursts. The sky adds a second planet, a station and a comet. The HUD sits in frames, with the ORB held shown as its icon.

The countdown

Today

The number shows in the HUD's text size. Nothing is heard: the race has no music and no effect.

After

3, 2, 1 and GO show large and pop in, each with its beep, and GO with its own sound. The engine hums from the first beat. The race tune starts at GO, so the beeps are heard clean. With reduced motion on, the numbers show without popping.

What you hear in one race

Today

The kart has no entry in the arcade's music list, and the race reports no event a sound could follow. Using an item makes no sound.

After

The race tune plays from GO, a FINAL LAP jingle speeds it up, and the arcade's fanfare plays the results. Each of your items, a box, a hit, a spin and a wall scrape has its sound. A rival's item sounds quieter and lower the farther it is, and not at all beyond 20 tiles. The engine's pitch follows your speed, and stops at the finish. Pausing stops the music, and M mutes everything.

What changes, what stays the same

Changes
  • The floor: metal, neon edges and kerbs, panel seams, a lunar verge, chevrons before each corner.
  • Around the track: pylons, beacons, the start arch, asteroids, satellites and wrecks, all on wall tiles.
  • Effects: shadows, sparks, the BOOST trail, hit bursts, the box flash, at most 64 particles.
  • The HUD: framed panels, the item as an icon, a big countdown.
  • The sky: a second planet, a station, comets.
  • Sound: a race tune and its faster final lap, an effect per item and per event, rival effects by distance, an engine hum.
  • Reduced motion: the sky, comets, satellites, boxes and beacons stand still, and no particle is drawn.
Stays the same
  • COMET RING's layout, its waypoints, its boxes and its start.
  • Driving: speeds, turning, the verge's half speed, walls.
  • Items and rivals: the draw, BOOST, BLOB, ORB, the CPU drivers.
  • The score and how it reaches the Hall of Heroes.
  • Loading: the kart's code still loads only when its cabinet opens.
  • Files: no image and no audio file; the art is drawn and the sound is synthesised.
  • M still mutes the arcade, and the choice is kept.
