title: Every open question in one Outbox, repository PRDs included
---
Work › Questions › Outbox now lists the open questions of repository PRDs beside those of product PRDs, in one list: the ones waiting on you first, then by rank, and the product filter covers both. A repository question shows when the server last read it (`as of <time>`) and links to its PRD page's Outbox tab, where you answer it as before; product questions are still answered in place.
