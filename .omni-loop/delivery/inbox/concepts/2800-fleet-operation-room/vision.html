    2 · 07:00. The laptop was closed all night. The Fleet opens on the Operation room: one "now" line per seat, then the ledger, newest first, every row signed by its seat — "Engineer · agent", never a first name. Nothing here imitates a person: no chat, no standup, no "I". Time first; the team in plain view.

    3 · 03:10. Reviewer · agent sends PR 2817 back. The Reviewer seat checks the Engineer's work before it becomes a real PR. Its hand-off is a typed verdict, pass or changes, with at most five findings, shown in full rather than as a count. A pair goes round at most twice; a finding seen twice stops the mission and asks a person.

    4 · 02:30. QA · agent fixes a bug itself. The person asked for this: QA may fix what it finds. To do it, QA takes the branch, so there is one writer at a time, then hands the branch back to the Engineer. The ledger shows who held the branch, and when.

    5 · Friday 09:02. A runner went silent. box-1 stopped answering. Today that job would simply fail and its worktree be swept away. In the Operation room the work carries on from its last save on box-3: a few minutes redone, nothing lost, nothing done twice. The clean-up line says what is still kept, by whom, until when, how big.

    6 · Waiting, said plainly. When the runners are full or the model provider's limit is reached, a seat waits instead of failing or looping. The room says what it waits for, until when, and that no tokens are spent meanwhile. Nothing to do; the one action is to be told when it moves.

    7 · PR 2817 is ready for review: 5 of 5. "Ready" is a claim with five proofs under it, each one tap away. Ready for review is not merged: the Engineer agent stops here, and only a person merges.

    8 · Seats. The crew list, agents first: who holds each seat, what it runs today, and its limits in sentences, so nobody guesses how far an agent goes. The PM and Architect seats are a person's today, and the page says why. From here the tour returns to the room.
