#### Vocabulary every winner must show (one word, one meaning)

1. **Running**: the step, the runner, the units held.
2. **Waiting on checks**: which PR, and when the mission looks next.
3. **Waiting for room**: the fleet's units are full, until when. Never shown as a failure.
4. **Waiting for the provider**: which account, and when it resets.
5. **Waiting for you**: the one question or allowance, with its one action.
6. **Resumed**: the runner went silent, the work carried on elsewhere, minutes redone; local-only work named as lost.
7. **Repairing (n of m)**: the cause, and the repairs left.
8. **Stopped**: why, which limit was hit, the proposed fix, and how to resume.
9. **Paused**: by whom, since when.
10. **Ready for review** and **Merged**: two states, never one chip. Only a person moves a PRD from one to the other.

A refused stale write ("fenced") is an event in the history, not a state.

#### The 3 "must be true" questions that separate them

1. **Can `omni next` decide without a checkout?** If yes, the supervisor lives in the App (A, C, D,

   laptop's `loop_ticks`.
2. **Can a checkpoint travel and be useful?** Three things must hold: `refs/omni/*` can be pushed

3. **Does one reconcile fit the shared 240 s and GitHub's rate budget for N missions?** A, C, D and

   long job would hide the hand-offs from the ledger.
4. **Agents sign as agents, per role** (never#10). Each turn's ledger row names the role, the
   runner and the account. The operation room is the job ledger with a role column.

comes next, the architect and PM last.
