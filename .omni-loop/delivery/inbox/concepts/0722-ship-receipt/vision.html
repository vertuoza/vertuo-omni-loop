Step 1 · The last merge

The last merge prints a receipt.

CI prints it on the pull request, because the merge itself prints nothing, and the same strip lands on the PRD’s page. It leads with what users get, only when it was measured. Then your part, what the loop did, and what’s out of stock, not billed.

  • Since round 2
  • Printed by CI on the PR, not by a terminal command.
  • It opens mid-print: the headline is already coming out.
  • “Approved by: nobody” is said once, not on every line.

Step 3 · Returns, 30 days

Object to any decision taken for you.

Pick one of the 14 decisions, write one line. A return is a product decision, so it opens a lite brainstorm, not /omni:bug-fix. The PRD stays shipped. A filed return shows how long it has waited, in plain sight, without a ping.

  • Since round 2
  • Returns go to a lite brainstorm, since bug-fix refuses product calls (Skeptic).
  • A waiting return shows its age: “no one on it · 6 days” (Craft).

Try it: tap a decision, or ↑↓ to pick and ⏎ to return it.

Step 4 · The credits skin

The credits play once, when you open it.

The first time you open a shipped PRD, its record plays as film credits. Any key skips, and it ends on the receipt. Later openings land straight on the receipt, and so does any opening after hours. Nothing plays on its own.

  • Since round 2
  • Kept as built: one record, two skins, the receipt stays the truth.

    Step 6 · Stand-up and team

    One line for stand-up. One receipt a month for leads.

    Your week copies as a small receipt image with the line beside it, so the thread gets the strip itself. The team receipt says how many ships were measured, links the specs that named no measure, and counts who started a PRD as a team total. Nothing ranks people.
