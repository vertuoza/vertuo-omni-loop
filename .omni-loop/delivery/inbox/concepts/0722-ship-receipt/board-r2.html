What the panel says about the prototypes

  • M (Morning Line) gets Fit 5 from all six. The Visionary dropped the round-2 dissent after seeing the room built: "small and honest, and it's the picture M lacked." Watch-out from the Skeptic, Craft and the developer: a start hook only prints, so y, ⏎ and u become messages Claude reads, and "you wrote that spec line" needs authorship that brainstorm does not record yet.
  • O (One Move to Building) gets Wow 5 and User value 5 from all six, and Feasibility 2 from all six. The live preview doesn't exist yet, and moving the phase-0 read to a STOP button needs its own PRD. Remixes: open on the preview, show labelled screenshots until previews exist, and let a PM steer from the phone.

Watch-outs: its first frame is an empty strip; `gh pr merge` prints nothing, so CI must print the receipt; and a return is a product decision, which /omni:bug-fix refuses, so the Skeptic routes it to a lite brainstorm.
