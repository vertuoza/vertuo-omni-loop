# Transcript — habit and adoption of the Omni Loop

## Round 1

### Turn 0 — what each panelist will look for

**Craft.** Four things. First, the first minute: does someone see a win before any setup step? Second, the Hemingway-bridge moment when a PRD ships: is the next step one keystroke? Third, empty, zero and error states: honest counts, no fake progress, always a way forward. Fourth, motion that stays inside the pixel grid, is quiet by default and never pings after hours.

**Visionary.** For each concept I will ask whether it would get screenshotted and sent to a colleague. The bar is a moment that shows the loop's value inside Claude Code in the first minute, without anyone clicking through a dashboard. I also want a real reason to open the page tomorrow: something shaped like Wrapped, a Hemingway bridge or Hades-style progress. I will refuse anything safe or grey, like a checklist, a nag or a leaderboard. Everything shown must be real data, and nothing may guilt anyone.

**User — developer.** Here's what I'll look for, as a dev on a product team. At 9am on Monday, does it show me one concrete next step that fits the gap after standup, on a laptop or a phone? Does it cut the merges and the /clear plus yolo-fix steps? Does it show real wins, meaning time saved and PRDs shipped? I'll mark down guilt pings, streaks, and any ranking of me by name.

**User — product manager.** I'm a PM, and most of my day is calls and Slack. In each concept I'll look for three things. First, one thing that's actually waiting for me that I can settle on my phone in two minutes at 8am, with no terminal. Second, an idea I can drop in mid-week that becomes a PRD without seven setup steps. Third, wins I can show my team. No guilt pings, streaks or leaderboards ranking me.

**Skeptic.** For each concept I'll ask what must be true. Which signal we already collect feeds it (no invented data, and no zero shown for a count nobody looked for)? Does the outbox gate stay red until a person answers? Does it avoid ranking individuals, guilt pings and unforgiving streaks? Do alerts stay opt-in? Can social features work when one person approves almost everything and the game is dormant? What's the smallest proof?

**Value.** The sharpest fact in the fuel sheet is that nearly every approval comes from one person. For each concept I'll ask: does it get a second and a tenth person to start a PRD this week? Does it give a reason to open the page tomorrow? Does it prove the payoff per team from signals already collected (release.md, merge time, adopted decisions), without ranking individuals or making up numbers?

### Moderator — no two concepts the same

Morning Docket (10× artist) sent back: it shares its direction with Mise en Place (a weekly box of kits prepped overnight from leftovers, on the page) and Mid-Sentence (the day left half-written in the terminal) — agents drafting the next PRD overnight — and differs from them in shade only. The 10× artist rewrites it in another direction.
