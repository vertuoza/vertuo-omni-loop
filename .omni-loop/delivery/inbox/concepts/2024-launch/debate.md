## Round 1 — eight concepts

### Moderator · the eight concepts

# Round 1 — the eight concepts (card files in this folder)

Shared by all: opt-in per PRD (at brainstorm or at ship; Omni may suggest, the PM decides), review and edit on the
PRD's page on the Omni page, nothing posts to #release-won without the PM's click.

**A · Written first** (contra-1.html) — lens contrarian
Wow: the PM reads the finished announcement before a line is built; on ship day it is already reviewed, footage in place.
Axis: WHEN — written at brainstorm (working backwards), the PM authors up front. 4-line script + Slack copy locked at
brainstorm; each script line is a footage slot filmed as slices merge/proof runs; narration+music rendered when slots
fill; on ship one light review, "Post to #release-won" (message + video in thread).

**B · Click-through** (contra-2.html) — lens contrarian
Wow: a sales rep clicks through the new feature in 40 s from the post's link, then replays the same demo live with a customer.
Axis: UNIT — no video; a clickable demo from the E2E moments (still + highlighted element + tooltip per step, ~5 steps)
+ short copy. Near-zero render/storage. PM reorders/edits steps, live Slack preview; post carries "Click through it".

**C · The Trailer Cut** (steal-1.html) — lens film trailers
Wow: #release-won shows a movie poster with a play button and a tagline instead of a paragraph.
Axis: STYLE — cinematic trailer, 4 beats (before, change, live clip, end card), key art poster, tagline, free voice +
music, "asked for by" cast. PM edits one line per beat; only that beat re-renders. "Premiere in #release-won".

**D · The Wire** (steal-2.html) — lens newsroom desk
Wow: the PM as editor picks one of three headline angles (benefit first, plain, pain first) and edits the lede in place,
seeing "As it will read" in Slack.
Axis: COPY LEADS — editorial copy + proof first, a 12 s clip secondary. "File to #release-won", or "Hold".

**E · The Direct** (delight-1.html) — lens delight, event cadence
Wow: every Friday 11:00 a short "This week in Vertuoza" show lands in #release-won; colleagues wait for it like a
Nintendo Direct.
Axis: CADENCE — weekly batch, one segment per opted-in launch; "Omni Direct #N" page with the rundown
(APPROVED/WAITING/OPTED OUT), auto-cut Thursday 18:00 with intro/outro/voice/music; chapters + per-segment clip, slide
and copy for sales/CS. Unapproved segment waits for next week.

**F · The Drop** (delight-2.html) — lens delight, the reader
Wow: a colleague scrolling #release-won sees a before/after wipe playing in the feed and gets the change before reading.
Axis: THE READER'S MOMENT — before/after wipe short from the E2E/proof recordings, hook line, "Watch 38 s", slide,
pitch line for sales/CS; "Approved by <PM>"; reactions and replies flow back to the PRD's retro.

**G · Script Cut** (a10x-1.html) — lens 10×
Wow: the PM rewrites one narration line on the Omni page and the matching clip re-cuts, re-voices and re-captions in
seconds — editing a launch feels like editing a sentence (Descript).
Axis: AUTHORING MODEL — the video is a script tied to clip segments; a facts-checked chip per claim vs the spec;
square, GIF, slide come along; one "Premiere in #release-won".

**H · Asked-for** (a10x-2.html) — lens 10×
Wow: the post opens "You asked. We shipped." and tags the sales/CS people whose customers asked, the day it ships.
Axis: DEMAND — built around who asked and who must act, not polish. Omni matches the PRD to requests it can see
(tickets, CS notes, User voice); PM ticks who to tag; "Post and tag"; the thread becomes the follow-up loop.
(Assumption: PRDs can be linked to customer requests — the PRD page has a User voice tab today.)

### skeptic · r1 t1

# Skeptic — round 1, turn 1

Question I ask of each concept: what must be true for this to work? Round 1 dial: no veto on cost, only conditions.

## What I checked in the repository first (facts the whole panel leans on)

- **The User voice tab is not customer demand.** `apps/galaxy/src/dossier/page/VoicePane.tsx` (PRD 822) draws
  *synthetic personas'* scores per round (persona:Irisa, persona:B-E DEv…), not tickets, CS notes or sales calls.
  Nothing in the repo links a PRD to a real customer request. H's stated assumption is false today.
- **No Slack post from the Omni page exists.** The only Slack code is the outbox note (`kit/lib/outbox/comment.ts`,
  `notify.slack` in `kit/lib/config.ts`): text written by a repo's CI job with a bot token secret. Nothing calls
  `chat.postMessage` or the 3-step `files.getUploadURLExternal` flow, and nothing runs server-side in the galaxy app
  with a Slack token. "Post to #release-won" from the page is net new, with a token held per workspace (`today`).
- **Filming has two different sources.** Proof clips (PRD 798) are Playwright on a *preview* deploy, ~3 s per
  criterion, test-shaped, WebM, in the `proof-videos` bucket (50 MB a file). The pitch walk (PRD 1108,
  `kit/lib/pitch/moments-film.ts`) films *production*, signed in with a stored `storage-state.json`, refusing any
  click that would change data. Both need a signed-in session somebody keeps alive.
- **Storage caps exist:** `PITCH_FILE_MAX_BYTES = 50 MB` (`kit/lib/pitch/push-run.ts`), `pitch-assets` 20 MB.
  Thomas's hand post attached a 12 MB .mov (`today`), so a compressed 30–40 s MP4 fits.
- **Rendering runs on a person's machine today** (local Claude session + ffmpeg, `/omni:pitch`). "Runs on its own
  after ship" means some machine renders without a person at the terminal.
- **Slack video:** inline playback of an uploaded MP4 is not documented (`references` › Hard constraints); the
  Video Block cannot take a raw file. Must be tested in Vertuoza's workspace before any concept promises "plays in
  the feed".

## Conditions every concept shares (I will not repeat them below)

1. **A renderer that runs without the PM's terminal.** CI runner or a hosted worker with a browser, ffmpeg and the
   free voice (Kokoro, `ref:12`) installed.
2. **A Slack app in Vertuoza's workspace** with `chat:write` + `files:write`, a member of #release-won, its token
   stored server-side for the Omni page to call. Who in Vertuoza approves that app?
3. **A signed-in film session on the product** (production for the pitch walk, preview for proof) that does not
   expire between ship and render.
4. **Feature visible where it is filmed at ship time.** Does "shipped" in Omni (feature PR merged into `main`)
   mean vertuo-apps' production has it? If production deploys later, or behind a flag, the production walk films
   the old screen.
5. **Trust in AI-written claims:** Thomas's post carried a limit ("creation date only since mid-September",
   `today`). That fact lives in data/backfill knowledge, maybe not in the spec. The draft must not drop limits it
   cannot see, and must never invent one.
