database

Functional

Today

A persona is excited, neutral or skeptical. Its trade is one lower-case word, so "data analyst" cannot be stored.

After

A persona is enthusiastic, neutral or skeptical; every excited persona now reads enthusiastic, and a writer that still says excited is understood. Every change to a persona is now recorded in the product's knowledge history, with who made it and what it said before and after, as Legal and Glossary changes are. A trade may be a few lower-case words, like "data analyst" or "credit-risk analyst".

Flow

Deploy order

  1. database: the migration (supabase workflow, on merge). Old app code keeps working: the functions still take excited.
  2. galaxy: Vercel, same merge. Until it is live, a read sends enthusiastic rows as stored; the old app's zod enum would refuse them, so the app ships in the same merge as the migration and the read maps before parsing.
  3. kit: the next release. Old kits keep reading through the mapping.
