Product knowledge on the Omni page: Legal and Glossary

PRD 1795 · P3 of the roadmap "Governed knowledge"

Functional

1. Where the knowledge lives

Today

repo A · registersrepo B · registers
  • Each repository keeps its own principles, rules and invariants.
  • A law's requirement would be copied into each repository, with no source and no owner.
  • Each repository has its own glossary form and its own avoided words.

After

Omni page · product Invoicing
Legal · Glossary→repo Arepo B
  • The product holds Legal entries (L-INVOICING-1, a sentence, a source law, an owner) and glossary terms (term, meaning, words to avoid).
  • A Legal owner and a Glossary owner, each with a delegate, edit them; other members propose.
  • Every repository of the product reads the same copy.

2. Who changes what

Today

Nobody: product knowledge does not exist on the Omni page.

After

WhoLegalGlossary
Workspace ownernames its owner and delegatenames its owner and delegate
Its owner or delegateadds, edits, retires; approves proposalsadds, edits, removes; approves proposals
Other memberproposesproposes
Outside the workspacereads nothingreads nothing

No owner named yet: the workspace's owners decide. A Legal entry is retired, never deleted; its id is never reused.

5. The words check

Today

no finding   # "refund" is on no list here

After

$ omni design words before-after.html
avoid list: design form + design.words.avoid
            + product glossary (local copy)
"refund": avoid, the product says "Credit note"

Read from the local copy only: the words check never calls the server.
