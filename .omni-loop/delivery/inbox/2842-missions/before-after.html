PRD 2842: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

The Fleet section (/app/fleet) only shows fleets: a row of fleet chips to pick one, the chosen fleet's name and mascot, its place this season, and its board (PRs merged, PRDs, repositories, questions answered, and a chart per day). It has no way to create a fleet. With no fleet, it says "This workspace has no fleet yet" and links to Settings › Fleets, where only a workspace owner creates one, and only a fleet of players for the game. A fleet of agents does not exist anywhere.

After

The Fleet section gets + New virtual fleet at the end of the fleet chips (and as the main button when there is no fleet yet). It opens a drawer on the right: the fleet's name first, then its icon (one of the fleet mascots), then its heroes: the five roles, each with a yes/no switch, all on by default, each saying whether it works alone today. Create fleet lands on the new fleet's Operation room: its heroes, idle, and an invitation to write its first mission. Its colour is picked for it and can be changed in Settings › Fleets.

Pixel perfect

screens: fleet-frame · fleet-new · fleet-room · fleet-seats · mission-start

omni-app

Functional

Today

The App runs checks, the retro and the knowledge jobs; nothing drives a roadmap.

After

The App runs each mission: at once on Start, a job's end, a merge or an answer, and every minute otherwise, sending the ready PRDs to the fleet's runners and recording every step.

Functional

Today

A person writes PRDs and roadmaps, and drives them from a laptop.

After

/omni:mission writes a mission's brief for a virtual fleet with a person (or in one line with --prd), commits it, and omni mission push puts it on its page.

Flow

Sequence (source)
person ─ /omni:mission ─▶ kit: mission.md + mission.html ─ omni mission push ─▶ galaxy: dossier kind mission + missions row (draft)
person ─ Start (Missions tab) ─▶ galaxy: status running, background_job_wake(mission) ─▶ supabase
omni-app (job ledger, lane mission:<id>) ─ reads missions, prd_stages, roadmap_prds, runner_jobs, plans ─▶ supabase
omni-app ─ insert runner_jobs /omni:yolo <prd> (sent_by_app, mission_key unique) + mission_events ─▶ supabase
runner ─ claims, builds, reports the end ─▶ galaxy ─ background_job_wake(mission) ─▶ omni-app runs again
omni-app ─ ends later +1 min ─▶ job ledger
