PRD 2842: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

The Fleet page is the fleet's board: who merged what this week. To build a roadmap, a person runs /omni:drive on a laptop and keeps it open; nothing records the goal the PRDs serve.

After

An owner creates a Virtual Fleet: a team of Virtual Heroes (PM, Engineer, Architect, QA, Reviewer by default), agents that run on the fleet's runners. A person then states a mission for that fleet (a goal, how to tell it worked, and its PRDs or roadmaps) and presses Start. The fleet takes the ready PRDs at once, several in parallel when they touch different files, and keeps going with the laptop closed, stopping only for an approval, an answer or a merge. The Missions tab says how far each mission is and what waits on you; the Operation room shows who did what, when, each row signed by its role.

omni-app

Functional

Today

The App runs checks, the retro and the knowledge jobs; nothing drives a roadmap.

After

The App runs each mission: at once on Start, a job's end, a merge or an answer, and every minute otherwise, sending the ready PRDs to the runners and recording every step.

Flow

Sequence (source)
person ─ /omni:mission ─▶ kit: mission.md + mission.html ─ omni mission push ─▶ galaxy: dossier kind mission + missions row (draft)
person ─ Start (Missions tab) ─▶ galaxy: status running, background_job_wake(mission) ─▶ supabase
omni-app (job ledger, lane mission:<id>) ─ reads missions, prd_stages, roadmap_prds, runner_jobs, plans ─▶ supabase
omni-app ─ insert runner_jobs /omni:yolo <prd> (sent_by_app, mission_key unique) + mission_events ─▶ supabase
runner ─ claims, builds, reports the end ─▶ galaxy ─ background_job_wake(mission) ─▶ omni-app runs again
omni-app ─ ends later +1 min ─▶ job ledger
