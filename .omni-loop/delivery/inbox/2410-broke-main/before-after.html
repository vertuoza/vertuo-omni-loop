PRD 2410: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

A shipped PRD's page ends at its Retro tab: the retro, when the delivery taught something, fourteen days after the merge at the latest. Nothing says whether the PRD broke main. The Engineering page counts pull requests, merges and the slices that needed a fix, but no PRD that broke main.

After

From the merge, the PRD's page says in its head Watching · 5 days left, then Clean, or Broke main the moment a red run on the merge commit, a revert or a regression lands, or Could not check when GitHub would not tell. A Main tab lists what broke, each with its link, then the rewind's findings: what happened, where it started (the repository, the Omni setup, or missing knowledge), the fix, and Approve or Reject.

The Engineering page's Loop health says, for the period, how many PRDs broke main out of those that finished their watch, and lists them.

omni-app

Functional

Today

When a feature PR merges, the App runs the retro, and fourteen days later its second run. Nothing watches main as a status.

After

Beside the retro, the App watches main for seven days in every repository the PRD merged into, and on the first break queues one /omni:rewind job, sent by the app.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the App has no screen.

supabase

Functional

Today

The database stores each PRD's stages, but nothing about what it did to main after the merge.

After

It stores each PRD's watch per repository, every signal that broke it, and every rewind finding with who approved or rejected it and when.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the database has no screen.

kit

Functional

Today

No command reads what a PRD did to main, and no skill walks a delivery back from a break.

After

/omni:rewind <n> reads the breaks (omni rewind facts), runs the seven checks from the break back to the spec, and sends its findings to the PRD page (omni rewind push). It writes nothing to the repository.

Pixel perfect

N/A — the kit has no screen.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. The migration (three tables, the approve and reject functions, their checks).
  2. The Omni App's main-watch function and the omni rewind endpoints.
  3. The kit (omni rewind, /omni:rewind), released with the next kit version.
  4. The Omni page: the status, the Main tab, the Engineering share.
