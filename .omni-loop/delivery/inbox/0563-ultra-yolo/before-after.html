What stays the same

  • An ordinary PRD: /omni:yolo, /omni:wave, /omni:yolo-fix and omni board behave exactly as today.
  • omni status, comment, answers, adopt and ship are unchanged: every item ends up in the plan repository.
  • Nothing merges into a default branch, in any repository; no label is created in a target.
  • Only a target's own committed preflight ever runs; a target without the loop is checked by its CI.
