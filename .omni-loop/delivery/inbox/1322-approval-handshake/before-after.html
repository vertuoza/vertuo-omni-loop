The approval handshake

Today a PRD born on the server waits silently until someone happens to open its page, and the run ends. After this PRD, the agent waits, the people who opted in are asked on their phone and by email, the approve screen shows what is being approved, the terminal and the HUD fire together, and a later change voids the approval on the spot.
