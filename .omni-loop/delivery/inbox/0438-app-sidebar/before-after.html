The app gets a sidebar

Today one top bar mixes three concerns. The workspace's work, Omni's own pages and your account all share a row, and most sections are not in it. After this PRD, a left sidebar holds where you can go: Work, then Omni. A top bar holds where you are and how you see it: the page's title, the theme switch, Game mode and your avatar.
