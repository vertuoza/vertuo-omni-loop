PRD 748 · from concept #746 · area business-core (the wedge)The business store

Omni Loop ships software for a business it knows nothing about: no agent knows who the product is sold to, or who it competes with, so every think-big brief designs for nobody in particular. This PRD gives each workspace one business with one or more products, made of claims with an id such as rival#4. A member fills it on a new Settings › Business tab by tapping choices, never typing unless the answer is missing. Agents read the confirmed claims with omni business show, and /omni:think-big cites them in its fuel sheet and logs what it cited. An empty workspace still works: every read says so in one line and exits 0.

Illustrative content. Vertuoza, ERP, construction, Belgium, Procore and the other rivals are examples only. Nothing is seeded: a new workspace starts empty, and no list in the code names a company.
