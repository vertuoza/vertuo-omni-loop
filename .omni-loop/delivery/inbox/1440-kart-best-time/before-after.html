The function decides the direction from the game; the browser never sends it. The same migration deletes the kart's point scores. A test fails the build if the SQL list of lowest-wins games and the registry's time games ever differ.

What changes, what stays

Stays the same
  • ENTROPY INVADERS and SUPER OMNI WORLD: their points, their end lines and their tables, highest first.
  • The race: driving, items, rivals and COMET RING's layout; the results table of six places and times.
  • Quitting from the pause still sends nothing.
  • Who may post: a player of the workspace, with the game unlocked, from 0 to 9,999,999.
  • Scores still earn no XP and no points in the galaxy.
  • The empty horizon is the next PRD; the cabinet's attract art is a separate visual fix.
Changes
  • The kart's measure is a time in tenths of a second, the lowest wins.
  • The results screen shows TIME 1:42.3 where it showed SCORE 1177.
  • The kart cabinet's CREW TOP 5 lists times, fastest first.
  • The kart's old point scores are deleted; the weekly exports already made keep them.
  • The guest's demo keeps the lowest time in the browser.
  • The place points and the 150-second par are gone from the kart's rules.
