PRD 1794 — before and after

PRD 1794 · P2 of the roadmap "Governed knowledge"

An unanswered owner escalates to a delegate, never to an auto-approve

Functional

Who is asked when a locked law or a high item waits on a person. The answer still has to come from a person: the gate stays red the whole time.

1. The config

Today

laws:
  source: knowledge
  owners: [pierrederval]
limits:
  stallDays: 5

One flat list of owners. Nothing names who stands in for them.

After

laws:
  source: knowledge
  owners:
    - login: pierrederval
      delegate: jdoe
  leadership: [cto-login]
limits:
  stallDays: 5
  escalationWorkingDays: 2

A plain login still works. With no delegate and no leadership, nothing changes.

2. A law-locked pull request while the owner is away

Today

  • Mon 10:00The outbox check goes red: law-locked, waiting for @pierrederval.
  • Wed 10:00Still red. Nobody is told.
  • Fri 10:00Still red. Nobody is told.
  • next Mon…Waits until the owner is back.

After

  • Mon 10:00 · step 0Red: law-locked. The App opens the escalation record on the pull request.
  • Wed 10:00 · step 1 (2 working days)One new comment asks the delegate. It shows in their bell on the Omni page.
    omni-loop[bot] commented
    @jdoe, you are asked in place of @pierrederval: N-PRODUCT-4 has waited 2 working days for a CODEOWNER's approval. Approve the head commit, or ask for changes. Nothing is approved for you.
  • Fri 10:00 · step 2 (2 more)One new comment asks @cto-login: reach an owner or the delegate, or change laws.owners.
  • when a CODEOWNER approvesGreen. Nobody more is asked.

3. A high item nobody answers

Today

  • day 0The feature PR stays draft. Only the PRD's opener sees the item in their bell.
  • any day afterSame people, same silence.

After

  • day 0 · step 0As today: the feature PR and the opener's bell.
  • +2 working days · step 1The owners in laws.owners and their delegates are mentioned, and see it in their bell.
  • +4 working days · step 2Product leadership too.
  • when a writer answersSettled as today. Nobody more is asked.

Owners and delegates, never leadership. omni check knowledge reports the block stale until it is rewritten.

The lines above are shortened; the block keeps every path PRD 1726 writes.

What stays the same

  • What clears law-locked: an approving review from a person CODEOWNERS lists, read from the base branch.
  • Who may answer an item: a writer of the repository, on the feature PR.
  • limits.stallDays, omni next and /omni:drive.
  • Medium items: adopted when raised, never escalated.

Pixel perfect: no screen changes
