kit CLI

Functional

TODAY

A runner starts Claude Code for every job, with the one Claude token it was given. When that account reaches its plan's 5-hour or weekly limit, the job stops with Claude's message and reads Failed · exit code 1. Every job after it fails the same way until the limit resets, and nobody is told why.

Codex and an open model cannot run a job, even when the workspace pays for them.

AFTER

The runner's owner lists its accounts, in order, in one file on the machine: two Claude accounts, one Codex, one OpenCode account with an open model hosted in the EU. Each account may be limited to some skills: the open model only takes pr-care, plan and bug-fix.

When an account runs out mid-job, the runner marks it out until its reset time, writes a handoff note, and the next ready account carries on in the same worktree. At most three switches; a crash never switches.

When every account is out, the runner pauses: Paused: no tokens left until 18:00, and takes no job until one is ready. Without the file, a runner works exactly as today.
