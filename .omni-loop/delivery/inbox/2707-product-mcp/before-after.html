PRD 2707: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

An agent in an editor reads the business, the claims, Legal, the Glossary and the knowledge graph through an agent link made on another page. It can’t read a business law, can’t read what an ADR decided, and can’t find anything without an id.

No product chooses whether agents read it.

After

An owner opens the product’s Settings, presses Turn on under MCP server, and copies its URL and one setup line.

A developer pastes that line with an API key the workspace already has. Their agent asks “what applies to API keys?” and gets the ADRs, rules, Legal entries and terms that mention it, across every repository of the product. It then reads the one it needs whole and cites its id.

An owner presses Turn off, and every agent stops reading on its next call.
