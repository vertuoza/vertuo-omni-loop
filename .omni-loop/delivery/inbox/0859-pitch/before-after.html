What stays the same

  • PRDs not yet shipped: no button, no tab, nothing new.
  • /omni:prove and the Proof tab are unchanged. A pitch posts no comment and changes no PR, issue or label.
  • Settings › Business keeps its products, claims and personas; Products only adds a page per product.
  • Nothing runs on the server: no renderer, no model key, no queue.
