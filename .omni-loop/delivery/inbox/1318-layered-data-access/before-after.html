PRD 1318 · before / after

Client → controller → service → repository

Nobody sees a new screen. What changes is how the galaxy app reaches its data: today nineteen browser files talk to Supabase themselves. After this PRD, the bell and the ask pages go through a controller, and a check keeps every new file on the same path.

The shape

A signed-in member answers a round on an ask page

After

  1. ask.client.tsPOST /api/ask/rounds/:id/answers, one screenshot per POST ≤ 4 MB
  2. ask.controller.tssession (cookie or terminal token) · zod-parse · call the service
  3. ask.service.tsthe round is open and yours to answer
  4. ask.repository.tsupload · update ask_rounds, as you (row-level security stays)

What the member sees does not change, except that a screenshot over 4 MB is refused before it is sent. The bucket's own limit drops from 5 MB to 4 MB.
