OMNI DUET: a point for every obstacle passed

PRD 2159. One chapter I wave of four obstacles (half bar, centre block, gate, half bar), and the score in the HUD as each one drops fully below the circle.

Today

  • +10 a wave passed in a chapter, +20 in endless mode; nothing else scores.
  • A full run of the four chapters: 400.

After

  • +1 an obstacle in a chapter, +2 in endless mode; finishing a wave adds nothing more.
  • A full run of the four chapters: 171, one per obstacle.

The same, before and after

  • A hit sets the score back to the chapter's starting score (in endless mode, the wave's): the points of obstacles already passed are given back, so no death earns a point.
  • The waves, the lives, the screens and the score's single send are unchanged.
  • Saved DUET scores stay on the leaderboard, on the old scale.
