kit

Functional

Today

A bug fix that sees a second problem writes it in its record's prose or in a Follow-up: line at the end of its run. Once the issue closes, nobody finds it.

A review reply says "Happy to take it as a follow-up" and resolves the thread. The promise is in no list.

After

Each open point is one file in .omni-loop/delivery/follow-ups/: the point, its source and the date. The fix or feature PR that saw it carries the file, so it merges with the fix.

omni bug refuses a record whose Still open point has no file. omni ship refuses a PRD whose "later" reply has no file. Nothing opens an issue or notifies anyone: the workspace's members decide. A later reply also asks a person, as a high outbox item, to agree to postpone it.
