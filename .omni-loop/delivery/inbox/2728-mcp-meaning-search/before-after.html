PRD 2728: before and after

galaxy

Functional

Today

An agent finds a business law only when it repeats the law's own words. On one real product, 2 of 12 everyday questions and 0 of 10 paraphrased ones found the right law in the top 5.

agent → search_knowledge {"query": "can a quote be sent without a payment term"}← {"hits": [ an unrelated ADR ], "total": 1}

Every word must appear in an entry: small words and plurals empty the result, or match the wrong entry.

After

An agent asks in its own words and gets the nearest laws, ADRs and rules first. The same product: the right law in the top 5 for 12 of 12 everyday questions and 8 of 10 paraphrased ones in the spike; the bar to ship is 11 and 7.

agent → search_knowledge {"query": "can a quote be sent without a payment term"}← {"mode": "meaning", "hits": [ BL-QUOTES-1 "A Quote with no Client, date, number or Payment Term can never be sent…", … ]}

The nearest entries by meaning come first; an exact id or term still comes first of all; when the model cannot answer, mode is words and the answer is today's.

Pixel perfect: no screen changes

omni-app

Functional

Today

On a merge, the App keeps the repository's knowledge as a pack. Nothing else happens.

After

After keeping the pack, the App turns each new or changed entry into a vector, by itself. Entries that did not change are not redone.

N/A — no screen: the App has none

database

Functional

Today

The product's knowledge is kept as files.

After

Beside the files, each workspace keeps a vector per entry text, readable only by the server. No person's login is ever turned into a vector, and vectors unused for 90 days are deleted.

Functional

Today

The entries agents read are built in the web app alone.

After

The same entries are built from the kit, so the App and the web app agree. Two ADRs sharing a number in one repository each get their own id.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. database: the migration (the vector extension, knowledge_vectors and its five functions)
  2. Edge Function embed: supabase functions deploy embed, in the supabase workflow, after the migrations
  3. kit: the moved entry builder, bundled into both apps
  4. omni-app: the knowledge-embed job and its queueing by the pack job
  5. galaxy: the new ranking; until vectors exist it answers mode: "words"

No coordinated deploy: each step works with the ones before it absent.
