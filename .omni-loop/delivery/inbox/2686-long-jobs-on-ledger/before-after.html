PRD 2686: before and after

omni-app

Functional

TODAY

You merge a feature PR. Inngest runs its retro, harvests its knowledge, and watches main for seven days.

Fourteen days later, the retro gains its "After merge" section. To get there, the run wakes every day, because Inngest caps a wait at seven days.

When the Inngest account has used its allowance, no retro is written, nothing is harvested, and a broken main is not noticed.

AFTER

You merge a feature PR. The App writes three jobs in its own ledger and runs them: the retro, the harvest, the main watch.

The retro waits once, for 14 days, then adds its "After merge" section. The watch looks once a day, and at once when a check goes red.

What you read is the same: the retro pull request or its verdict comment, the harvest, the watch's status and the /omni:rewind it queues. A job that failed can be replayed.

Flow

Deploy order

  1. PRD 2685 is merged and live.
  2. Merge: the migration applies through the supabase workflow.
  3. The App deploys. Inngest is sent nothing more.
  4. Within the hour, the comparison adds a job for each retro owed (merges of the last 14 days) and each lane still watching.
  5. Runs still waiting inside Inngest are left to end there: their functions are no longer served.
