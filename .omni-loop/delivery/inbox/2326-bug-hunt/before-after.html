BUG HUNT · PRD 2326

The arcade's sixth game, unlocked at LV 6: a first-person shooter in a maze made of a codebase's modules. The player walks, turns and fires at waves of bugs, with three lives, for the crew's score table.

Five games, the last opening at LV 5. None puts the player inside the world: they look from the side, from above or from behind a kart.

The match and the raycaster are pure and seeded, so the same presses give the same game. The code loads only when the cabinet opens and adds no dependency.
