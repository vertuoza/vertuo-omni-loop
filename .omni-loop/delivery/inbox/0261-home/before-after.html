Strategy guide: how the loop works

1-1Brainstorm

You and Claude turn an idea into an approved PRD.

1-2Plan

The PRD is cut into thin slices, grouped in waves.

1-3Waves

One agent per slice, in its own worktree, test-first, with its own pull request.

1-4Outbox

Every decision taken without asking is written down. You answer once, at the end.

1-5Ship

The feature pull request is ready, and a person merges it.

★ BONUSKnowledge

Merged decisions land in the knowledge base, so the next loop knows more.
