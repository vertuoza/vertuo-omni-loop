PRD 451 · GALAXY ARCADE · DESKTOP

Fullscreen is opt-in on desktop

Today the arcade asks the browser for fullscreen on the first click or key of every page load, so a desktop player loses their tabs and address bar the moment they touch anything. After, the window stays as it is. A small, dim ⛶ in the page's corner (or F) takes it fullscreen when the player wants it. Phones keep today's behaviour.
